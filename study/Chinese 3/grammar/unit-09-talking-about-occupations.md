# Chinese 3 · Unit 9: 谈职业 (Tán zhíyè) — Talking about Occupations

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 153, 159–163, 166–169 (printed pp. 139, 145–149, 152–155)

This unit is about jobs, part-time work and future plans. Grammar: (1) 看起来 (kàn qǐlai) / 听起来 (tīng qǐlai) “it looks like / it sounds…”; (2) 一来…… (yìlái……)，二来…… (èrlái……) “for one thing…, for another…”. The self-test also checks how to talk about jobs (当 (dāng) + job, 在 (zài) + place + 工作 (gōngzuò)). Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 看起来、听起来 (kàn qǐlai, tīng qǐlai) — The structures 看起来 (kàn qǐlai) and 听起来 (tīng qǐlai)

“<ruby>看<rt>Kàn</rt></ruby><ruby>起来<rt>qǐlai</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用作<rt>yòngzuò</rt></ruby><ruby>插入语<rt>chārùyǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>揣摩<rt>chuǎimó</rt></ruby>、<ruby>估计<rt>gūjì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“看起来 (kàn qǐlai)” is used as a parenthesis (an inserted phrase) to show a guess or estimate, e.g.,

1. <ruby>天<rt>Tiān</rt></ruby><ruby>阴<rt>yīn</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>看<rt>kàn</rt></ruby><ruby>起来<rt>qǐlai</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>下<rt>xià</rt></ruby><ruby>雨<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   It's clouded over — looks like it's going to rain.

“<ruby>听<rt>Tīng</rt></ruby><ruby>起来<rt>qǐlai</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>通过<rt>tōngguò</rt></ruby><ruby>听<rt>tīng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>得到<rt>dédào</rt></ruby><ruby>某种<rt>mǒuzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>印象<rt>yìnxiàng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“听起来 (tīng qǐlai)” means getting an impression from what you hear, e.g.,

2. <ruby>他<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>听<rt>tīng</rt></ruby><ruby>起来<rt>qǐlai</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>对<rt>duì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   What he said sounds right.

> **Pattern:** <ruby>看<rt>kàn</rt></ruby><ruby>起来<rt>qǐlai</rt></ruby>／<ruby>听<rt>tīng</rt></ruby><ruby>起来<rt>qǐlai</rt></ruby> + <ruby>判断<rt>pànduàn</rt></ruby>　·　<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>看<rt>kàn</rt></ruby><ruby>起来<rt>qǐlai</rt></ruby>／<ruby>听<rt>tīng</rt></ruby><ruby>起来<rt>qǐlai</rt></ruby> + <ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby>  
> looks like / sounds like + (your judgement) — at the start of the clause, or right after the thing described

> 💡 看起来 (kàn qǐlai) = judging from what you see or the situation; 听起来 (tīng qǐlai) = judging from what you've heard. Put it at the start of the judgement: ✓ 看起来我们的周末会很忙 (kàn qǐlai wǒmen de zhōumò huì hěn máng) / ✓ 听起来他说得对 (tīng qǐlai tā shuōdéduì). ✗ 我们的周末会很忙看起来 (wǒmen de zhōumò huì hěn máng kàn qǐlai), ✗ 他听起来说得对 (tā tīng qǐlai shuōdéduì).

### 2. 一来……，二来…… (yī lái……, èr lái……) — The structure 一来…… (yìlái……)，二来…… (èrlái……)

<ruby>关联词<rt>Guānliáncí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>连接<rt>liánjiē</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>原因<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>目的<rt>mùdì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>每<rt>měi</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>开头<rt>kāitóu</rt></ruby>。<ruby>有时候<rt>Yǒushíhou</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>继续<rt>jìxù</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>三<rt>sān</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby>……，<ruby>四<rt>sì</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby>……”。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
A connective linking clauses that give reasons or purposes; it goes at the start of each clause. Sometimes it continues with “三来…… (sān lái……)，四来…… (sì lái……)”, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** ……，<ruby>一<rt>yī</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby> + <ruby>原因<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby><ruby>1<rt>yī èr</rt></ruby>，<ruby>二<rt>lái</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby> + <ruby>原<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby><ruby>因<rt>èr</rt></ruby>2  
> …, for one thing + reason 1, for another + reason 2

1. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>北京<rt>Běijīng</rt></ruby><ruby>留<rt>liú</rt></ruby><ruby>学<rt>xué</rt></ruby>，<ruby>一<rt>yī</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby><ruby>因为<rt>yīnwèi</rt></ruby><ruby>北京<rt>Běijīng</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>大学<rt>dàxué</rt></ruby>，<ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>名胜<rt>míngshèng</rt></ruby><ruby>古迹<rt>gǔjì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>二<rt>èr</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby><ruby>听说<rt>tīngshuō</rt></ruby><ruby>北京<rt>Běijīng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>留学生<rt>liúxuéshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>交到<rt>jiāodào</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>国家<rt>guójiā</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>朋友<rt>péngyou</rt></ruby>。  
   I want to study in Beijing: for one thing, it has great universities and many historic sites; for another, I hear there are lots of international students there, so I can make friends from many countries.

> 💡 一来 (yìlái) / 二来 (èrlái) open their clauses — never at the end (✗ 可以赚一点儿钱一来 (kěyǐ zhuàn yìdiǎnr qián yìlái)).

### 3. 职业的说法 (zhíyè de shuōfǎ) — Talking about jobs

> 💡 Not a numbered grammar note, but tested in the self-test (result C). “Be/become a…” for a job is 当 (dāng) or 是一名 (shì yìmíng); “work at…” puts 在 (zài) + place before 工作 (gōngzuò).

1. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>当<rt>dāng</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>名<rt>míng</rt></ruby><ruby>外交官<rt>wàijiāoguān</rt></ruby>。  
   I want to be a diplomat. (not ✗ 我的理想是医生 (wǒ de lǐxiǎng shì yīshēng) for “my dream job is…”)

2. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>家<rt>jiā</rt></ruby><ruby>旅行社<rt>lǚxíngshè</rt></ruby><ruby>工作<rt>gōngzuò</rt></ruby>。  
   I work at a travel agency. (not ✗ 我工作在一家旅行社 (wǒ gōngzuò zài yìjiā lǚxíngshè))

3. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>名<rt>míng</rt></ruby><ruby>国际<rt>guójì</rt></ruby><ruby>导游<rt>dǎoyóu</rt></ruby>，<ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>工作<rt>gōngzuò</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>带着<rt>dàizhe</rt></ruby><ruby>外国<rt>wàiguó</rt></ruby><ruby>游客<rt>yóukè</rt></ruby><ruby>游览<rt>yóulǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>北京<rt>Běijīng</rt></ruby>。  
   I'm an international tour guide; my job is taking foreign tourists around Beijing.
