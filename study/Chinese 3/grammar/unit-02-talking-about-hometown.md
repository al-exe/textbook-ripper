# Chinese 3 · Unit 2: 谈家乡 (Tán jiāxiāng) — Talking about Hometown

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 34, 40–41, 43–47 (printed pp. 17, 23–24, 26–31)

This unit is about introducing a city or your hometown. Grammar: (1) verb reduplication — 看看 (kànkan), 介绍介绍 (jièshào jièshào) (“have a look”, “give a quick introduction”); (2) 够……的 (gòu……de) “really/quite…”; (3) 别看…… (bié kàn……)，……可…… (kě……) “don't be fooled by…, actually…”. Also practised: ……是……之一 (shì……zhīyī) “one of the…”. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 动词重叠 (dòngcí chóngdié) — Reduplication of a verb

<ruby>汉语<rt>Hànyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>里<rt>li</rt></ruby><ruby>有些<rt>yǒuxiē</rt></ruby><ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>重叠<rt>chóngdié</rt></ruby>，<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>动作<rt>dòngzuò</rt></ruby><ruby>经历<rt>jīnglì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>时间<rt>shíjiān</rt></ruby><ruby>短<rt>duǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>轻松<rt>qīngsōng</rt></ruby>、<ruby>随便<rt>suíbiàn</rt></ruby>；<ruby>有时<rt>yǒushí</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>尝试<rt>chángshì</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>礼貌<rt>lǐmào</rt></ruby>。<ruby>单音节<rt>Dānyīnjié</rt></ruby><ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>重叠<rt>chóngdié</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>形式<rt>xíngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>“AA”<ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby>“A<ruby>一<rt>yi</rt></ruby>A”，<ruby>如<rt>rú</rt></ruby>“<ruby>看<rt>kàn</rt></ruby>（<ruby>一<rt>yi</rt></ruby>）<ruby>看<rt>kàn</rt></ruby>”“<ruby>听<rt>tīng</rt></ruby>（<ruby>一<rt>yi</rt></ruby>）<ruby>听<rt>tīng</rt></ruby>”“<ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>（<ruby>一<rt>yi</rt></ruby>）<ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>”。<ruby>双音节<rt>Shuāngyīnjié</rt></ruby><ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>重叠<rt>chóngdié</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>形式<rt>xíngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>“ABAB”，<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>为<rt>wéi</rt></ruby>“AB<ruby>一<rt>yi</rt></ruby>AB”，<ruby>如<rt>rú</rt></ruby>“<ruby>介绍<rt>jièshào</rt></ruby><ruby>介绍<rt>jièshào</rt></ruby>”“<ruby>休息<rt>xiūxi</rt></ruby><ruby>休息<rt>xiūxi</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>为<rt>wéi</rt></ruby>“<ruby>介绍<rt>jièshào</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yi</rt></ruby><ruby>介绍<rt>jièshào</rt></ruby>”“<ruby>休息<rt>xiūxi</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yi</rt></ruby><ruby>休息<rt>xiūxi</rt></ruby>”。  
In Chinese, some verbs can be reduplicated to indicate an action is short, easy or casual; sometimes it also indicates a try or shows courtesy. The reduplicative form of a monosyllabic verb is “AA” or “A一 (yī)A”, for example “看 (kàn)（一 (yī)）看 (kàn)”, “听 (tīng)（一 (yī)）听 (tīng)”, “说 (shuō)（一 (yī)）说 (shuō)”. The reduplicative form of a disyllabic verb is “ABAB” rather than “AB一 (yī)AB”, for example “介绍介绍 (jièshào jièshào)”, “休息休息 (xiūxi xiūxi)”, but not “介绍一介绍 (jièshào yí jièshào)”, “休息一休息 (xiūxi yì xiūxi)”.

> **Pattern:** AA／A<ruby>一<rt>yi</rt></ruby>A　·　ABAB  
> 看看 (kànkan) kànkan / 看一看 (kàn yi kàn) kàn yi kàn · 介绍介绍 (jièshào jièshào) jièshào jièshào (never 介绍一介绍 (jièshào yí jièshào))

1. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>看看<rt>kànkan</rt></ruby><ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>书<rt>shū</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>？  
   Can I have a look at your book?

2. <ruby>我们<rt>Wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>休息<rt>xiūxi</rt></ruby><ruby>休息<rt>xiūxi</rt></ruby><ruby>吧<rt>ba</rt></ruby>。  
   Let's take a little rest.

<ruby>动词<rt>Dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>重叠<rt>chóngdié</rt></ruby><ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>再<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>带<rt>dài</rt></ruby><ruby>补语<rt>bǔyǔ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>我们<rt>Wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>“<ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>看看<rt>kànkan</rt></ruby><ruby>一下<rt>yíxià</rt></ruby>”。  
If a verb is reduplicated, it is not followed by a complement. “我看看一下 (wǒ kànkan yíxià)” is wrong.

<ruby>动词<rt>Dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>重叠<rt>chóngdié</rt></ruby><ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>带<rt>dài</rt></ruby><ruby>宾语<rt>bīnyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>时<rt>shí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>若<rt>ruò</rt></ruby><ruby>宾语<rt>bīnyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>前<rt>qián</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>数量<rt>shùliàng</rt></ruby><ruby>词语<rt>cíyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>定语<rt>dìngyǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>这个<rt>zhège</rt></ruby><ruby>数量<rt>shùliàng</rt></ruby><ruby>词语<rt>cíyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>应该<rt>yīnggāi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>定指<rt>dìngzhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>，<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>确定<rt>quèdìng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>量<rt>liàng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>我们<rt>Wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>“<ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>看看<rt>kànkan</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>本<rt>běn</rt></ruby><ruby>书<rt>shū</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>应该<rt>yīnggāi</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>“<ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>看看<rt>kànkan</rt></ruby><ruby>那<rt>nà</rt></ruby><ruby>本<rt>běn</rt></ruby><ruby>书<rt>shū</rt></ruby>”。  
When a verb is reduplicated and followed by an object, and there is a quantifier used before the object as an attributive, the quantifier should refer to something definite rather than something indefinite. “我看看那本书 (wǒ kànkan nàběnshū)” is correct, but “我看看一本书 (wǒ kànkan yì běnshū)” is wrong.

> 💡 Reduplication = “a bit / have a go”. So it already means “a little”: don't add 一下 (yíxià) (✗ 介绍介绍一下 (jièshào jièshào yíxià)), and the object should be specific (✓ 看看那本书 (kànkan nàběnshū), ✗ 看看一本书 (kànkan yì běnshū)).

### 2. 够……的 (gòu…… de) — The structure 够……的 (gòu……de)

“<ruby>够<rt>Gòu</rt></ruby>……<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>”<ruby>结构<rt>jiégòu</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>用来<rt>yònglái</rt></ruby><ruby>强调<rt>qiángdiào</rt></ruby><ruby>程度<rt>chéngdù</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>高<rt>gāo</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The structure “够……的 (gòu……de)” is used to stress that the degree is high, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>真<rt>zhēn</rt></ruby>／<ruby>可<rt>kě</rt></ruby>）<ruby>够<rt>gòu</rt></ruby> + <ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby> + <ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>  
> really / quite + adjective (the 的 (de) at the end is needed)

1. <ruby>汉语<rt>Hànyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>真<rt>zhēn</rt></ruby><ruby>够<rt>gòu</rt></ruby><ruby>难<rt>nán</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   Chinese is really hard.

2. <ruby>今天<rt>Jīntiān</rt></ruby><ruby>够<rt>gòu</rt></ruby><ruby>冷<rt>lěng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   It's pretty cold today.

3. <ruby>从<rt>Cóng</rt></ruby><ruby>这儿<rt>zhèr</rt></ruby><ruby>到<rt>dào</rt></ruby><ruby>那儿<rt>nàr</rt></ruby><ruby>可<rt>kě</rt></ruby><ruby>够<rt>gòu</rt></ruby><ruby>远<rt>yuǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   It's quite a long way from here to there.

### 3. 别看……，……可…… (bié kàn……, …… kě……) — The structure 别看…… (bié kàn……)，……可…… (kě……)

“<ruby>别<rt>Bié</rt></ruby><ruby>看<rt>kàn</rt></ruby>……，……<ruby>可<rt>kě</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>常用<rt>chángyòng</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby><ruby>承认<rt>chéngrèn</rt></ruby><ruby>别人<rt>biéren</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>观点<rt>guāndiǎn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但<rt>dàn</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>提出<rt>tíchū</rt></ruby><ruby>不同<rt>bùtóng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>观点<rt>guāndiǎn</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The structure “别看…… (bié kàn……)，……可…… (kě……)” is used to agree with others' opinion, but also to raise a different opinion, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>别<rt>bié</rt></ruby><ruby>看<rt>kàn</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>别人<rt>biérén</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>看法<rt>kànfǎ</rt></ruby>），+ ……<ruby>可<rt>kě</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>不同<rt>bùtóng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>看法<rt>kànfǎ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>／<ruby>着<rt>zhe</rt></ruby><ruby>呢<rt>ne</rt></ruby>  
> 别看 (bié kàn) + (what others think), … 可 (kě) + (the opposite point) + 了 (le)/着呢 (zhe ne)

1. <ruby>别<rt>Bié</rt></ruby><ruby>看<rt>kàn</rt></ruby><ruby>她<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>美国<rt>Měiguó</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>，<ruby>她<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>汉语<rt>Hànyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>可<rt>kě</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   She may be American, but her Chinese is really good.

2. <ruby>别<rt>Bié</rt></ruby><ruby>看<rt>kàn</rt></ruby><ruby>房间<rt>fángjiān</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>小<rt>xiǎo</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可<rt>kě</rt></ruby><ruby>舒服<rt>shūfu</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   The room may be small, but it's really comfortable.

> 💡 别看 (bié kàn) is followed by the other person's view (the room is small); 可 (kě) introduces your contrasting view (but it's comfortable). 可……了 (kě……le) / 可……着呢 (kě……zhe ne) = “really …”.

### 4. ……是……之一 (…… shì …… zhī yī) — … is one of the …

> 💡 Not a numbered grammar note in the book, but drilled in this unit (“之一 (zhīyī)”吧 (ba)): 之一 (zhīyī) goes at the end of the noun phrase — 北京的名胜古迹之一 (Běijīng de míngshènggǔjì zhīyī) “one of Beijing's famous sites”. Often with 最 (zuì): 最有名的……之一 (zuì yǒumíng de……zhīyī) “one of the most famous …”.

1. <ruby>故宫<rt>Gùgōng</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>北京<rt>Běijīng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>名胜<rt>míngshèng</rt></ruby><ruby>古迹<rt>gǔjì</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>zhī</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yī</rt></ruby>，<ruby>非常<rt>fēicháng</rt></ruby><ruby>有名<rt>yǒumíng</rt></ruby>。  
   The Forbidden City is one of Beijing's historic sites; it's very famous.
