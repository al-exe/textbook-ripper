# Chinese 3 · Unit 5: 谈考试 (Tán kǎoshì) — Talking about an Examination

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 85, 91–97 (printed pp. 69, 75–81)

This unit is about exams, studying and how you feel about them. Grammar: (1) 连……也 (lián……yě)/都…… (dōu……) “even…”; (2) ……是…… (shì……)，不过…… (búguò……) “it's true that…, but…”; (3) 哪怕……也…… (nǎpà……yě……) “even if…, still…”. Also: 可不 (kěbù) “exactly / you said it”. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 连……也/都…… (lián…… yě / dōu……) — The structure 连……也 (lián……yě)/都…… (dōu……)

“<ruby>连<rt>Lián</rt></ruby>……<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>/<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>汉语<rt>Hànyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>强调<rt>qiángdiào</rt></ruby><ruby>句式<rt>jùshì</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>连<rt>Lián</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>强调<rt>qiángdiào</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>对象<rt>duìxiàng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>后面<rt>hòumian</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>”<ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby>“<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby>”<ruby>与<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>zhī</rt></ruby><ruby>呼应<rt>hūyìng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>意思<rt>Yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>强调<rt>qiángdiào</rt></ruby><ruby>极端<rt>jíduān</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>对象<rt>duìxiàng</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>典型<rt>diǎnxíng</rt></ruby><ruby>代表<rt>dàibiǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby><ruby>尚且<rt>shàngqiě</rt></ruby><ruby>如此<rt>rúcǐ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>其他<rt>qítā</rt></ruby><ruby>对象<rt>duìxiàng</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>更<rt>gèng</rt></ruby><ruby>不用说<rt>búyòngshuō</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“连……也 (lián……yě)/都…… (dōu……)” is one of the Chinese emphatic sentence patterns. Followed by what is to be emphasized and often with “也 (yě)” or “都 (dōu)”, “连 (lián)” means even something extreme or typical is like this, let alone other cases, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>连<rt>lián</rt></ruby> + <ruby>极端<rt>jíduān</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>／<ruby>事物<rt>shìwù</rt></ruby> + <ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>／<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby>  
> even + (the most extreme case) + 也 (yě)/都 (dōu) + verb

1. <ruby>他<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>忙<rt>máng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>连<rt>lián</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby><ruby>饭<rt>fàn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>时间<rt>shíjiān</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby>。  
   He's so busy he doesn't even have time to eat.

2. <ruby>这个<rt>Zhège</rt></ruby><ruby>汉字<rt>Hànzì</rt></ruby><ruby>连<rt>lián</rt></ruby><ruby>老师<rt>lǎoshī</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>认识<rt>rènshi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>学生<rt>xuésheng</rt></ruby><ruby>更<rt>gèng</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>认识<rt>rènshi</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   Even the teacher doesn't know this character, let alone the students.

> 💡 Put the most extreme/unlikely example after 连 (lián): 这个字连小孩子都认识 (zhège zì lián xiǎoháizi dōu rènshi) “even little kids know this character” (so of course I do). Switching the roles (✗ 小孩子连这个字都认识 (xiǎoháizi lián zhège zì dōu rènshi)) makes the character the “extreme” case and loses the point.

### 2. ……是……，不过…… (…… shì……, búguò……) — The structure ……是…… (shì……)，不过…… (búguò……)

“……<ruby>是<rt>Shì</rt></ruby>……，<ruby>不过<rt>búguò</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>结构<rt>jiégòu</rt></ruby><ruby>用以<rt>yòngyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>承认<rt>chéngrèn</rt></ruby><ruby>一种<rt>yìzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>事实<rt>shìshí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但<rt>dàn</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>提出<rt>tíchū</rt></ruby><ruby>另<rt>lìng</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>观点<rt>guāndiǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>事实<rt>shìshí</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The structure “……是…… (shì……)，不过…… (búguò……)” is used to acknowledge a fact and also propose another idea or fact, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** A<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>A，<ruby>不过<rt>búguò</rt></ruby>……  
> (it's) A, all right, but… — the same word on both sides of 是 (shì)

1. <ruby>汉字<rt>Hànzì</rt></ruby><ruby>难<rt>nán</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>难<rt>nán</rt></ruby>，<ruby>不过<rt>búguò</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby>。  
   Chinese characters are hard, sure, but they're very interesting.

2. <ruby>吃<rt>Chī</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>不过<rt>búguò</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>现在<rt>xiànzài</rt></ruby><ruby>又<rt>yòu</rt></ruby><ruby>饿<rt>è</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   I did eat, but I'm hungry again now.

3. <ruby>看<rt>Kàn</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>看见<rt>kànjiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>不过<rt>búguò</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>。  
   I did see it, but I can't say.

> 💡 Repeat the verb/adjective around 是 (shì): 好吃是好吃 (hǎochī shì hǎochī) / 看是看过 (kàn shì kànguò). The concession (A) comes first and your real point after 不过 (búguò): ✓ 好吃是好吃 (hǎochī shì hǎochī)，不过有点儿油 (búguò yǒudiǎnr yóu); ✗ 好吃是有点儿油 (hǎochī shì yǒudiǎnr yóu)，不过好吃 (búguò hǎochī).

### 3. 让步复句：哪怕……也…… (ràngbù fùjù: nǎpà…… yě……) — Concession compound sentence: 哪怕……也…… (nǎpà……yě……)

“<ruby>哪怕<rt>Nǎpà</rt></ruby>……<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>但<rt>dàn</rt></ruby><ruby>已经<rt>yǐjīng</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>预见<rt>yùjiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>结果<rt>jiéguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>哪怕<rt>Nǎpà</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后面<rt>hòumian</rt></ruby><ruby>接<rt>jiē</rt></ruby><ruby>最<rt>zuì</rt></ruby><ruby>极端<rt>jíduān</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>可能性<rt>kěnéngxìng</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后面<rt>hòumian</rt></ruby><ruby>常常<rt>chángcháng</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>决定<rt>juédìng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“哪怕……也…… (nǎpà……yě……)” indicates something that hasn't happened but whose result is already predictable. “哪怕 (nǎpà)” is followed by the most extreme possibility, and “也 (yě)” is often followed by a decision, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>哪怕<rt>nǎpà</rt></ruby> + <ruby>最<rt>zuì</rt></ruby><ruby>极端<rt>jíduān</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby>，（<ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby>）<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby> + <ruby>决定<rt>juédìng</rt></ruby>  
> even if + (worst case), (I) still + (decision)

1. <ruby>哪怕<rt>Nǎpà</rt></ruby><ruby>熬夜<rt>áoyè</rt></ruby>，<ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>把<rt>bǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>作业<rt>zuòyè</rt></ruby><ruby>做完<rt>zuòwán</rt></ruby>。  
   Even if I have to stay up all night, I'll finish my homework.

2. <ruby>哪怕<rt>Nǎpà</rt></ruby><ruby>再<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>累<rt>lèi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>坚持<rt>jiānchí</rt></ruby>。  
   However tired I get, I'll keep going.

> 💡 The hardship goes after 哪怕 (nǎpà), the decision after 也 (yě): ✓ 哪怕熬夜 (nǎpà áoyè)，我也要做完 (wǒ yě yào zuò wán); ✗ 哪怕做完 (nǎpà zuò wán)，我也要熬夜 (wǒ yě yào áoyè).
