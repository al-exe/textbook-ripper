# Chinese 3 · Unit 1: 谈人物特征 (Tán rénwù tèzhēng) — Talking about the Characteristics of a Person

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 18, 24–26, 29–32, 200 (printed pp. 1, 7–9, 12–15, 186)

This unit is about describing people — looks, age, height, hair and personality. Grammar: (1) ……，只是…… (zhǐshì……) “…, only/but…” (good point first, drawback second); (2) 既……又…… (jì……yòu……) “both… and…”; (3) reduplicated adjectives (大大的眼睛 (dàdàde yǎnjing), 高高兴兴 (gāogāoxìngxìng)). Translations and answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet; the listening transcript and its answer come from the book's appendix.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 转折复句：……，只是…… (zhuǎnzhé fùjù: ……, zhǐshì……) — Transitional compound sentence: ……，只是…… (zhǐshì……)

“<ruby>只是<rt>Zhǐshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>表<rt>biǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>转折<rt>zhuǎnzhé</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>关联<rt>guānlián</rt></ruby><ruby>词语<rt>cíyǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>前边<rt>qiánbian</rt></ruby><ruby>通常<rt>tōngcháng</rt></ruby><ruby>为<rt>wéi</rt></ruby><ruby>积极意义<rt>jījíyìyì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby><ruby>通常<rt>tōngcháng</rt></ruby><ruby>为<rt>wéi</rt></ruby><ruby>消极<rt>xiāojí</rt></ruby><ruby>意义<rt>yìyì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“只是 (zhǐshì)” is a conjunction indicating transition, usually with the former part indicating positive meanings and the latter part indicating negative meanings, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>方面<rt>fāngmiàn</rt></ruby>），<ruby>只是<rt>zhǐshì</rt></ruby>（<ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>太<rt>tài</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>方面<rt>fāngmiàn</rt></ruby>）  
> (good point), 只是 (zhǐshì) (drawback) — “…, it's just that…”

1. <ruby>她<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>长<rt>zhǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>真<rt>zhēn</rt></ruby><ruby>漂亮<rt>piàoliang</rt></ruby>，<ruby>只是<rt>zhǐshì</rt></ruby><ruby>个子<rt>gèzi</rt></ruby><ruby>有点儿<rt>yǒudiǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>矮<rt>ǎi</rt></ruby>。  
   She's really pretty, only she's a bit short.

2. <ruby>今天<rt>Jīntiān</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>天气<rt>tiānqì</rt></ruby><ruby>不错<rt>búcuò</rt></ruby>，<ruby>只是<rt>zhǐshì</rt></ruby><ruby>听说<rt>tīngshuō</rt></ruby><ruby>晚上<rt>wǎnshang</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>雨<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby>。  
   The weather is nice today, but I hear it will rain tonight.

> 💡 Keep the order: positive first, negative after 只是 (zhǐshì). “昨天的菜真好吃 (zuótiān de cài zhēn hǎochī)，只是有点儿少 (zhǐshì yǒudiǎnr shǎo)” (tasty, only a bit little) is right; flipping it (“真少 (zhēn shǎo)，只是很好吃 (zhǐshì hěn hǎochī)”) sounds wrong. 只是 (zhǐshì) is softer than 但是 (dànshì)/可是 (kěshì).

### 2. 并列复句：既……又…… (bìngliè fùjù: jì…… yòu……) — Coordinate compound sentence: 既……又…… (jì……yòu……)

“<ruby>既<rt>Jì</rt></ruby>……<ruby>又<rt>yòu</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>连接结构<rt>liánjiējiégòu</rt></ruby><ruby>和<rt>hé</rt></ruby><ruby>音节<rt>yīnjié</rt></ruby><ruby>数目<rt>shùmù</rt></ruby><ruby>相同<rt>xiāngtóng</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>相似<rt>xiāngsì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby>、<ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>及<rt>jí</rt></ruby><ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>词组<rt>cízǔ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“既……又…… (jì……yòu……)” is used to connect adjectives, verbs or verb phrases with similar structures or the same number of syllables, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>既<rt>jì</rt></ruby> + A + <ruby>又<rt>yòu</rt></ruby> + B  
> both A and B (A and B have the same structure)

1. <ruby>他<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>既<rt>jì</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>朋友<rt>péngyou</rt></ruby>，<ruby>又<rt>yòu</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>老师<rt>lǎoshī</rt></ruby>。  
   He is both my friend and my teacher.

2. <ruby>她<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>既<rt>jì</rt></ruby><ruby>热情<rt>rèqíng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>又<rt>yòu</rt></ruby><ruby>开朗<rt>kāilǎng</rt></ruby>。  
   She is both warm and cheerful.

<ruby>否定<rt>Fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>形式<rt>xíngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>“<ruby>既<rt>jì</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby>“<ruby>既<rt>jì</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby>……<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby>……”。  
Its negative form is “既不……也不…… (jì bù……yě bù……)” or “既没……也没…… (jì méi……yě méi……)” (from the book's self-test explanation).

3. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>那儿<rt>nàr</rt></ruby><ruby>既<rt>jì</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>吃过<rt>chīguo</rt></ruby><ruby>意大利<rt>Yìdàlì</rt></ruby><ruby>菜<rt>cài</rt></ruby>，<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>吃过<rt>chīguo</rt></ruby><ruby>法国<rt>Fǎguó</rt></ruby><ruby>菜<rt>cài</rt></ruby>。  
   There I've eaten neither Italian food nor French food.

> 💡 Both halves must match: 既会说汉语 (jì huì shuō Hànyǔ)，又会做中国菜 (yòu huì zuò Zhōngguó cài) (both 会 (huì) + verb). You can't mix a positive and a negative half (✗ 既会说汉语 (jì huì shuō Hànyǔ)，不会做中国菜 (bú huì zuò Zhōngguó cài)), and 没 (méi) goes after 既 (jì), not before it (✗ 没既吃过 (méi jì chī guo)).

### 3. 形容词重叠做定语 (xíngróngcí chóngdié zuò dìngyǔ) — Reduplication of an adjective used as an attributive

<ruby>形容词<rt>Xíngróngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>重叠<rt>chóngdié</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>加深<rt>jiāshēn</rt></ruby><ruby>程度<rt>chéngdù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>同时<rt>tóngshí</rt></ruby><ruby>带有<rt>dàiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>一定<rt>yídìng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>感情<rt>gǎnqíng</rt></ruby><ruby>色彩<rt>sècǎi</rt></ruby>。<ruby>单音节<rt>Dānyīnjié</rt></ruby><ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>重叠<rt>chóngdié</rt></ruby><ruby>形式<rt>xíngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>为<rt>wéi</rt></ruby>“AA”，<ruby>如<rt>rú</rt></ruby>“<ruby>黑黑的<rt>hēihēide</rt></ruby><ruby>头发<rt>tóufa</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>双<rt>shuāng</rt></ruby><ruby>音节<rt>yīnjié</rt></ruby><ruby>重叠<rt>chóngdié</rt></ruby><ruby>形式<rt>xíngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>为<rt>wéi</rt></ruby>“AABB”，<ruby>如<rt>rú</rt></ruby>“<ruby>高高兴兴<rt>gāogāoxìngxìng</rt></ruby>”、“<ruby>漂漂亮亮<rt>piàopiàoliàngliàng</rt></ruby>”。<ruby>形容词<rt>Xíngróngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>重叠<rt>chóngdié</rt></ruby><ruby>形式<rt>xíngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>定语<rt>dìngyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>前边<rt>qiánbian</rt></ruby><ruby>不再<rt>búzài</rt></ruby><ruby>受<rt>shòu</rt></ruby><ruby>程度<rt>chéngdù</rt></ruby><ruby>副词<rt>fùcí</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>修饰<rt>xiūshì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
An adjective can be reduplicated for intensification to show certain emotion. The reduplicative form of a monosyllabic adjective is “AA”, for example “黑黑的头发 (hēihēide tóufa)”; and the reduplicative form of a disyllabic adjective is “AABB”, for example “高高兴兴 (gāogāoxìngxìng)”, “漂漂亮亮 (piàopiàoliàngliàng)”. If an adjective is reduplicated to serve as an attributive, it is not modified by an adverb of degree preceding it, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** AA<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby> + <ruby>名词<rt>míngcí</rt></ruby> ／ AABB<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>  
> 黑 (hēi) → 黑黑的头发 (hēihēide tóufa); 高兴 (gāoxìng) → 高高兴兴的 (gāogāoxìngxìng de)

1. <ruby>她<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>双<rt>shuāng</rt></ruby><ruby>大大<rt>dàdà</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>眼睛<rt>yǎnjing</rt></ruby>。  
   She has a pair of big, big eyes.

2. <ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby><ruby>北京<rt>Běijīng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>大龙<rt>Dàlóng</rt></ruby><ruby>每<rt>měi</rt></ruby><ruby>天<rt>tiān</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>高高兴兴<rt>gāogāoxìngxìng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   In Beijing, Dalong is happy every day.

> 💡 No 很 (hěn)/非常 (fēicháng)/真 (zhēn) in front of a reduplicated adjective — the doubling already means “very”: ✓ 长长的黑发 (chángchángde hēifà), ✗ 很长长的黑发 (hěn chángchángde hēifà); ✓ 非常漂亮 (fēicháng piàoliang), ✗ 非常漂漂亮亮的 (fēicháng piàopiàoliàngliàng de).
