## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 一……就…… (yī…… jiù……) — The structure 一……就…… (yī……jiù……)

“<ruby>一<rt>Yī</rt></ruby>……<ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>两件<rt>liǎngjiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>紧接着<rt>jǐnjiēzhe</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“一……就…… (yī……jiù……)” indicates two events happening one immediately after the other, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>一<rt>yī</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动作<rt>dòngzuò</rt></ruby>A，（<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动作<rt>dòngzuò</rt></ruby>B  
> (subject) + as soon as A, (subject) + 就 (jiù) + B — subjects go before 一 (yī) and before 就 (jiù)

1. <ruby>他们<rt>Tāmen</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>下<rt>xià</rt></ruby><ruby>课<rt>kè</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>商店<rt>shāngdiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   They went to the shop as soon as class ended.

2. <ruby>他<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>打算<rt>dǎsuan</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>考完<rt>kǎowán</rt></ruby><ruby>试<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>回<rt>huí</rt></ruby><ruby>国<rt>guó</rt></ruby>。  
   He plans to go home as soon as he finishes his exams.

<ruby>有时<rt>Yǒushí</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>一<rt>yī</rt></ruby>……<ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>前<rt>qián</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>条件<rt>tiáojiàn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>结果<rt>jiéguǒ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
Sometimes the first clause states a condition and the second the result (“whenever…”), e.g.,

3. <ruby>一<rt>Yí</rt></ruby><ruby>到<rt>dào</rt></ruby><ruby>周末<rt>zhōumò</rt></ruby>，<ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>叫上<rt>jiàoshang</rt></ruby><ruby>几<rt>jǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>朋友<rt>péngyou</rt></ruby><ruby>一起<rt>yìqǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>郊外<rt>jiāowài</rt></ruby><ruby>登<rt>dēng</rt></ruby><ruby>山<rt>shān</rt></ruby>、<ruby>攀岩<rt>pānyán</rt></ruby>。  
   Whenever the weekend comes, he gets some friends together to go climbing outside the city.

4. <ruby>一<rt>Yí</rt></ruby><ruby>到<rt>dào</rt></ruby><ruby>寒假<rt>hánjià</rt></ruby>、<ruby>暑假<rt>shǔjià</rt></ruby>，<ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>出去<rt>chūqu</rt></ruby><ruby>旅行<rt>lǚxíng</rt></ruby>。  
   Every winter and summer holiday, he goes travelling.

“<ruby>就<rt>Jiù</rt></ruby>”<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>副词<rt>fùcí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>一般<rt>yìbān</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>前边<rt>qiánbian</rt></ruby>。  
“就 (jiù)” is an adverb, usually placed right before the verb.

> 💡 ✓ 他一喝酒 (tā yì hējiǔ)，就头疼 (jiù tóuténg) / ✓ 他一到冬天就感冒 (tā yí dào dōngtiān jiù gǎnmào). ✗ 一他喝酒 (yì tā hējiǔ) / ✗ 就他头疼 (jiù tā tóuténg) — the subject never comes after 一 (yī) or 就 (jiù).

### 2. 恨不得 (hènbude) — The use of 恨不得 (hènbude)

“<ruby>恨不得<rt>Hènbude</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>非常<rt>fēicháng</rt></ruby><ruby>急切<rt>jíqiè</rt></ruby><ruby>地<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>希望<rt>xīwàng</rt></ruby>（<ruby>实现<rt>shíxiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>某事<rt>mǒushì</rt></ruby>）。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“恨不得 (hènbude)” means to wish desperately (to do something), e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>恨不得<rt>hènbude</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>马上<rt>mǎshàng</rt></ruby>／<ruby>现在<rt>xiànzài</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>短语<rt>duǎnyǔ</rt></ruby>  
> subject + is dying to + (right now) + do something — must be followed by the action

1. <ruby>他<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>整天<rt>zhěngtiān</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>喜欢<rt>xǐhuan</rt></ruby><ruby>宅<rt>zhái</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>家<rt>jiā</rt></ruby><ruby>里<rt>li</rt></ruby><ruby>玩儿<rt>wánr</rt></ruby><ruby>游戏<rt>yóuxì</rt></ruby>、<ruby>写<rt>xiě</rt></ruby><ruby>博客<rt>bókè</rt></ruby>，<ruby>恨不得<rt>hènbude</rt></ruby><ruby>顿顿<rt>dùndùn</rt></ruby><ruby>饭<rt>fàn</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>叫<rt>jiào</rt></ruby><ruby>外卖<rt>wàimài</rt></ruby>。  
   He just wants to stay home all day gaming and blogging; he'd love to order takeaway for every meal.

2. <ruby>最近<rt>Zuìjìn</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>忙<rt>máng</rt></ruby><ruby>极了<rt>jíle</rt></ruby>，<ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>恨不得<rt>hènbude</rt></ruby><ruby>每<rt>měi</rt></ruby><ruby>天<rt>tiān</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>睡<rt>shuì</rt></ruby><ruby>觉<rt>jiào</rt></ruby>，<ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>早<rt>zǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>点儿<rt>diǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>把<rt>bǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>工作<rt>gōngzuò</rt></ruby><ruby>做完<rt>zuòwán</rt></ruby>。  
   He's extremely busy lately — he'd skip sleep every day if it meant finishing the work sooner.

> 💡 The subject comes before 恨不得 (hènbude) (✓ 她恨不得明天就回国 (tā hènbude míngtiān jiù huíguó), ✗ 恨不得她… (hènbude tā…)), and 恨不得 (hènbude) can't stand alone as an answer (✗ 我恨不得 (wǒ hènbude)。) — say what you're dying to do.

### 3. 被动句 (bèidòngjù) — Passive sentences

<ruby>被动<rt>Bèidòng</rt></ruby><ruby>句<rt>jù</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>指<rt>zhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>与<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>谓语<rt>wèiyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>之间<rt>zhījiān</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>被动<rt>bèidòng</rt></ruby><ruby>关系<rt>guānxi</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>即<rt>jí</rt></ruby><ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>谓语<rt>wèiyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>所<rt>suǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>行为<rt>xíngwéi</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>受<rt>shòu</rt></ruby><ruby>动<rt>dòng</rt></ruby><ruby>者<rt>zhě</rt></ruby>。<ruby>被动<rt>Bèidòng</rt></ruby><ruby>句<rt>jù</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>遭受<rt>zāoshòu</rt></ruby>、<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>情愿<rt>qíngyuàn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意义<rt>yìyì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>由<rt>Yóu</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>被动<rt>bèidòng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>介词<rt>jiècí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>及其<rt>jíqí</rt></ruby><ruby>宾语<rt>bīnyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>状语<rt>zhuàngyǔ</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>作用<rt>zuòyòng</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>引出<rt>yǐnchū</rt></ruby><ruby>动作<rt>dòngzuò</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>发出<rt>fāchū</rt></ruby><ruby>者<rt>zhě</rt></ruby>（<ruby>施事者<rt>shīshìzhě</rt></ruby>），<ruby>如果<rt>rúguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>清楚<rt>qīngchu</rt></ruby><ruby>或者<rt>huòzhě</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>需要<rt>xūyào</rt></ruby><ruby>强调<rt>qiángdiào</rt></ruby><ruby>动作<rt>dòngzuò</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>发出<rt>fāchū</rt></ruby><ruby>者<rt>zhě</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>宾语<rt>bīnyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>省略<rt>shěnglüè</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
A passive sentence is one where the subject receives the action of the verb. It often carries a sense of suffering something or of it happening against one's will. The preposition “被 (bèi)” and its object act as an adverbial; “被 (bèi)” introduces the doer of the action. If the doer is unknown or unimportant, the object after “被 (bèi)” can be left out, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>受事者<rt>shòushìzhě</rt></ruby> + <ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>施事者<rt>shīshìzhě</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby> + <ruby>结果<rt>jiéguǒ</rt></ruby>／<ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>  
> receiver + 被 (bèi) + (doer) + verb + result/了 (le)

1. <ruby>那<rt>Nà</rt></ruby><ruby>双<rt>shuāng</rt></ruby><ruby>鞋<rt>xié</rt></ruby><ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby>（<ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby>）<ruby>穿坏<rt>chuānhuài</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   Those shoes were worn out (by him).

2. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>自行车<rt>zìxíngchē</rt></ruby><ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby>（<ruby>朋友<rt>péngyou</rt></ruby>）<ruby>骑走<rt>qízǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   My bike was ridden off (by a friend).

3. <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>我们<rt>wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意见<rt>yìjiàn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>我们<rt>wǒmen</rt></ruby>“<ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>代表<rt>dàibiǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   This isn't our opinion — we were “represented” without being asked.

<ruby>口语<rt>Kǒuyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>常用<rt>chángyòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>让<rt>ràng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby>“<ruby>叫<rt>jiào</rt></ruby>”<ruby>代替<rt>dàitì</rt></ruby>“<ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>但<rt>dàn</rt></ruby>“<ruby>让<rt>ràng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>和<rt>hé</rt></ruby>“<ruby>叫<rt>jiào</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>宾语<rt>bīnyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>省略<rt>shěnglüè</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
In spoken Chinese “被 (bèi)” is often replaced by “让 (ràng)” or “叫 (jiào)”, but the object after “让 (ràng)” or “叫 (jiào)” can't be left out, e.g.,

4. <ruby>那<rt>Nà</rt></ruby><ruby>双<rt>shuāng</rt></ruby><ruby>鞋<rt>xié</rt></ruby><ruby>让<rt>ràng</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>穿坏<rt>chuānhuài</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   Those shoes were worn out by him.

5. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>自行车<rt>zìxíngchē</rt></ruby><ruby>叫<rt>jiào</rt></ruby><ruby>朋友<rt>péngyou</rt></ruby><ruby>骑走<rt>qízǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   My bike was taken by a friend.

“……<ruby>被<rt>Bèi</rt></ruby>（……）<ruby>称作<rt>chēngzuò</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>有时<rt>yǒushí</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>“……<ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby>（……）<ruby>叫作<rt>jiàozuò</rt></ruby>……”，<ruby>前者<rt>qiánzhě</rt></ruby><ruby>语气<rt>yǔqì</rt></ruby><ruby>更<rt>gèng</rt></ruby><ruby>正式<rt>zhèngshì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“……被 (bèi)（……）称作…… (chēngzuò……)” can also be said as “……被 (bèi)（……）叫作…… (jiàozuò……)”; the former is more formal, e.g.,

6. <ruby>艾娜<rt>Àinà</rt></ruby><ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby><ruby>朋友们<rt>péngyoumen</rt></ruby><ruby>称作<rt>chēngzuò</rt></ruby>“<ruby>麦霸<rt>màibà</rt></ruby>”。  
   Edna is called “karaoke queen” by her friends.

<ruby>否定<rt>Fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>时<rt>shí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>否定词<rt>fǒudìngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>介词<rt>jiècí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>前<rt>qián</rt></ruby>。  
To negate, put the negative word before the preposition “被 (bèi)”.

7. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>被<rt>bèi</rt></ruby><ruby>雨<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>淋湿<rt>línshī</rt></ruby>。  
   I didn't get soaked by the rain.

> 💡 ✓ 自行车没被大龙骑走 (zìxíngchē méi bèi Dàlóng qí zǒu) — ✗ 自行车被大龙没骑走 (zìxíngchē bèi Dàlóng méi qí zǒu).
