# Chinese 3 · Unit 7: 谈假日经历 (Tán jiàrì jīnglì) — Talking about Experiences on a Day Off

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 117, 124–126, 130–131, 133–134 (printed pp. 101, 108–110, 114–115, 117–118)

This unit is about what you did on your days off and how it felt. Grammar: (1) the particle 来着 (láizhe) (something that happened recently / something you've forgotten); (2) 不但…… (búdàn……)，还…… (hái……) “not only…, but also…”; (3) rhetorical questions with 哪……啊 (nǎ……a)？ “how could…?”. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 助词“来着” (zhùcí “láizhe”) — The particle 来着 (láizhe)

“<ruby>来着<rt>Láizhe</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>已经<rt>yǐjīng</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>过<rt>guo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事<rt>shì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>末尾<rt>mòwěi</rt></ruby>。<ruby>用<rt>Yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>来着<rt>láizhe</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>否定<rt>fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>形式<rt>xíngshì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby><ruby>疑问句<rt>yíwènjù</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby>”、“<ruby>哪<rt>nǎ</rt></ruby>”、“<ruby>谁<rt>shéi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>等<rt>děng</rt></ruby><ruby>疑问<rt>yíwèn</rt></ruby><ruby>代词<rt>dàicí</rt></ruby><ruby>构成<rt>gòuchéng</rt></ruby><ruby>特指<rt>tèzhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>疑问句<rt>yíwènjù</rt></ruby><ruby>进行<rt>jìnxíng</rt></ruby><ruby>提问<rt>tíwèn</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“来着 (láizhe)” is used at the end of a sentence to indicate something that already happened. A sentence with “来着 (láizhe)” has no negative form. In a question, an interrogative pronoun such as “什么 (shénme)”, “哪 (nǎ)” or “谁 (shéi)” can be used, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** ……（<ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>短语<rt>duǎnyǔ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>来着<rt>láizhe</rt></ruby>  
> (what happened) + 来着 (láizhe) — always at the very end, never negative

1. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>跟<rt>gēn</rt></ruby><ruby>小丽<rt>Xiǎolì</rt></ruby><ruby>一起<rt>yìqǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>雅秀<rt>Yǎxiù</rt></ruby><ruby>市场<rt>shìchǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>来着<rt>láizhe</rt></ruby>。  
   I went to Yaxiu Market with Xiaoli.

2. <ruby>刚才<rt>Gāngcái</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>跟<rt>gēn</rt></ruby><ruby>朋友<rt>péngyou</rt></ruby><ruby>踢<rt>tī</rt></ruby><ruby>足球<rt>zúqiú</rt></ruby><ruby>来着<rt>láizhe</rt></ruby>。  
   I was just playing football with friends.

3. <ruby>你们<rt>Nǐmen</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>哪儿<rt>nǎr</rt></ruby><ruby>买<rt>mǎi</rt></ruby><ruby>东西<rt>dōngxi</rt></ruby><ruby>来着<rt>láizhe</rt></ruby>？  
   Where did you go shopping?

“<ruby>来着<rt>Láizhe</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用于<rt>yòngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>特指<rt>tèzhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>问句<rt>wènjù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>原来<rt>yuánlái</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby><ruby>可是<rt>kěshì</rt></ruby><ruby>现在<rt>xiànzài</rt></ruby><ruby>想不起来<rt>xiǎngbuqǐlái</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
In a question with a question word, “来着 (láizhe)” can also mean you knew something but can't remember it now, e.g.,

4. <ruby>你<rt>Nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>姐姐<rt>jiějie</rt></ruby><ruby>叫<rt>jiào</rt></ruby><ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby><ruby>名字<rt>míngzi</rt></ruby><ruby>来着<rt>láizhe</rt></ruby>？  
   What was your sister's name again?

5. “Shūyíng”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>“yíng”<ruby>字<rt>zì</rt></ruby><ruby>怎么<rt>zěnme</rt></ruby><ruby>写<rt>xiě</rt></ruby><ruby>来着<rt>láizhe</rt></ruby>？  
   How do you write the “yíng” in “Shūyíng” again?

> 💡 来着 (láizhe) goes last, after any 了 (le): the book's self-test marks 昨天你去哪儿了来着 (zuótiān nǐ qù nǎr le láizhe)？ as right and 去哪儿来着了 (qù nǎr láizhe le) as wrong. You can't negate it (✗ 没去来着 (méi qù láizhe)).

### 2. 递进复句：不但……，还…… (dìjìn fùjù: búdàn……, hái……) — Progressive compound sentence: 不但…… (búdàn……)，还…… (hái……)

<ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>不但<rt>búdàn</rt></ruby>……，<ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>复句<rt>fùjù</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby>”<ruby>引出<rt>yǐnchū</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>所<rt>suǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>比<rt>bǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>前<rt>qián</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>所<rt>suǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>更<rt>gèng</rt></ruby><ruby>进一步<rt>jìnyíbù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>或者<rt>huòzhě</rt></ruby><ruby>补充<rt>bǔchōng</rt></ruby><ruby>更<rt>gèng</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>信息<rt>xìnxī</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
In a compound sentence with “不但…… (búdàn……)，还…… (hái……)”, the clause introduced by “还 (hái)” goes further than the previous clause or adds more information, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>不但<rt>búdàn</rt></ruby> + A，（<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby> + B  
> subject + not only A, (subject) + also B

1. <ruby>她<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>不但<rt>búdàn</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby><ruby>唱<rt>chàng</rt></ruby><ruby>歌<rt>gē</rt></ruby>，<ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby><ruby>写<rt>xiě</rt></ruby><ruby>歌<rt>gē</rt></ruby>。  
   She can not only sing but also write songs.

2. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>不但<rt>búdàn</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby><ruby>她<rt>tā</rt></ruby>，（<ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby>）<ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>见过<rt>jiànguo</rt></ruby><ruby>她<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>呢<rt>ne</rt></ruby>。  
   I don't just know of her — I've even met her.

<ruby>当<rt>Dāng</rt></ruby><ruby>两<rt>liǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>相同<rt>xiāngtóng</rt></ruby><ruby>时<rt>shí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby>“<ruby>不但<rt>búdàn</rt></ruby>”<ruby>前边<rt>qiánbian</rt></ruby>，<ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>省略<rt>shěnglüè</rt></ruby>，<ruby>若<rt>ruò</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>省略<rt>shěnglüè</rt></ruby>，<ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>一定<rt>yídìng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby>“<ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>前边<rt>qiánbian</rt></ruby>，<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby>。  
When both clauses have the same subject, the subject goes before “不但 (búdàn)”, and it may be left out in the second clause. If it isn't left out, it must come before “还 (hái)”, not after.

> 💡 ✓ 她不但爱逛街 (tā búdàn ài guàngjiē)，还爱登山 (hái ài dēngshān). ✗ 不但她爱逛街… (búdàn tā ài guàngjiē…). ✓ 她不但会做中国菜 (tā búdàn huì zuò Zhōngguó cài)，她还会做法国菜 (tā hái huì zuò fǎguócài). ✗ …，还她会做法国菜 (hái tā huì zuò fǎguócài).

### 3. 反问句：哪……啊？ (fǎnwènjù: nǎ…… a?) — Rhetorical question: 哪……啊 (nǎ……a)？

<ruby>反问句<rt>Fǎnwènjù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>作用<rt>zuòyòng</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>加强<rt>jiāqiáng</rt></ruby><ruby>语气<rt>yǔqì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>把<rt>bǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>本来<rt>běnlái</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>明确<rt>míngquè</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>看法<rt>kànfǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>表现<rt>biǎoxiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>更加<rt>gèngjiā</rt></ruby><ruby>鲜明<rt>xiānmíng</rt></ruby>、<ruby>强烈<rt>qiángliè</rt></ruby>。<ruby>反问句<rt>Fǎnwènjù</rt></ruby><ruby>分为<rt>fēnwéi</rt></ruby><ruby>肯定<rt>kěndìng</rt></ruby><ruby>反问句<rt>fǎnwènjù</rt></ruby><ruby>和<rt>hé</rt></ruby><ruby>否定<rt>fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>反问句<rt>fǎnwènjù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>肯定<rt>kěndìng</rt></ruby><ruby>反问句<rt>fǎnwènjù</rt></ruby><ruby>表达<rt>biǎodá</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>否定<rt>fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>否定<rt>fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>反问句<rt>fǎnwènjù</rt></ruby><ruby>表达<rt>biǎodá</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>肯定<rt>kěndìng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby>。<ruby>主语<rt>Zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby>“<ruby>哪<rt>nǎ</rt></ruby>……<ruby>啊<rt>a</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>前边<rt>qiánbian</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
A rhetorical question strengthens the tone, making an already clear opinion more vivid and forceful. An affirmative rhetorical question means the negative; a negative one means the positive. The subject goes before “哪……啊 (nǎ……a)”, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>哪<rt>nǎ</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby>／<ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby>……<ruby>啊<rt>a</rt></ruby>？  
> subject + 哪 (nǎ) + verb… 啊 (a)? = “how could…?” (means the opposite)

1. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>哪<rt>nǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby><ruby>啊<rt>a</rt></ruby>？（<ruby>强调<rt>Qiángdiào</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby>）  
   How would I know? (= I don't know)

2. <ruby>他<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>哪<rt>nǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby><ruby>啊<rt>a</rt></ruby>？（<ruby>强调<rt>Qiángdiào</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby>）  
   How could he not know? (= he knows)

3. <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>哪<rt>nǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>锻炼<rt>duànliàn</rt></ruby><ruby>身体<rt>shēntǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>啊<rt>a</rt></ruby>？（<ruby>强调<rt>Qiángdiào</rt></ruby><ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>锻炼<rt>duànliàn</rt></ruby><ruby>身体<rt>shēntǐ</rt></ruby>）  
   How is this exercise? (= this isn't exercise)

> 💡 哪 (nǎ) comes after the subject: ✓ 他哪会瑜伽啊 (tā nǎ huì yújiā a)？ ✗ 哪他会瑜伽啊 (nǎ tā huì yújiā a)？. Often followed by a correction with 才……呢 (cái……ne): 逛街哪能锻炼身体啊 (guàngjiē nǎ néng duànliànshēntǐ a)？登山才锻炼身体呢 (dēngshān cái duànliànshēntǐ ne)！
