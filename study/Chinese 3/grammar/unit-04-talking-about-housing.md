# Chinese 3 · Unit 4: 谈住房 (Tán zhùfáng) — Talking about Housing

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 63, 69–83 (printed pp. 47, 53–67)

This unit teaches three structures: (1) sentences of existence with 有 (yǒu), 是 (shì) and 在 (zài); (2) the 的 (de) phrase (红的 (hóng de) "the red one", 吃的 (chī de) "something to eat"); (3) 什么 (shénme)（+noun）都…… (dōu……) "every… / any…". Read the grammar notes, do the practice, then check the answer key at the bottom. Book pinyin is used wherever the textbook prints it; translations and model answers marked as such were written for this study sheet.

## 重点语句 (Zhòngdiǎn yǔjù) — Key sentences

2. <ruby>楼<rt>Lóu</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shàng</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>三<rt>sān</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>房间<rt>fángjiān</rt></ruby>。  
   There are three rooms upstairs.

3. <ruby>厨房<rt>Chúfáng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>客厅<rt>kètīng</rt></ruby><ruby>旁边<rt>pángbiān</rt></ruby>。

4. <ruby>沙发<rt>Shāfā</rt></ruby>、<ruby>床<rt>chuáng</rt></ruby>、<ruby>电视<rt>diànshì</rt></ruby>、<ruby>冰箱<rt>bīngxiāng</rt></ruby>、<ruby>洗衣机<rt>xǐyījī</rt></ruby>……<ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby>。  
   Sofa, bed, TV, fridge, washing machine… it has everything.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 存在句 (cúnzài jù) — Sentences indicating existence

<ruby>动词<rt>Dòngcí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby>”、“<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>”、“<ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby>”<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>存在<rt>cúnzài</rt></ruby>，<ruby>它们<rt>tāmen</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>谓语<rt>wèiyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>时<rt>shí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>语序<rt>yǔxù</rt></ruby><ruby>分别<rt>fēnbié</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>：  
The verbs “有 (yǒu)”, “是 (shì)” and “在 (zài)” can be used to indicate existence. If used as the predicate, the word order of the sentence is as follows:

> **Pattern:** （1）<ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>处<rt>chù</rt></ruby>——<ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby>/<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>——<ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>（<ruby>物<rt>wù</rt></ruby>）  
> location — 有 (yǒu)/是 (shì) — somebody (something)

> **Pattern:** （2）<ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>（<ruby>物<rt>wù</rt></ruby>）——<ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby>——<ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>处<rt>chù</rt></ruby>  
> somebody (something) — 在 (zài) — location

1. <ruby>楼<rt>Lóu</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shàng</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>三<rt>sān</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>房间<rt>fángjiān</rt></ruby>。  
   There are three rooms upstairs.

2. <ruby>北边<rt>Běibian</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>书房<rt>shūfáng</rt></ruby>。  
   To the north is the study.

3. <ruby>厨房<rt>Chúfáng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>客厅<rt>kètīng</rt></ruby><ruby>旁边<rt>pángbiān</rt></ruby>。  
   The kitchen is next to the living room.

<ruby>用<rt>Yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>存在<rt>cúnzài</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>跟<rt>gēn</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>存在<rt>cúnzài</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>不同之处<rt>bùtóngzhīchù</rt></ruby><ruby>在于<rt>zàiyú</rt></ruby>：  
Both a sentence with “有 (yǒu)” and a sentence with “是 (shì)” can be used to indicate existence. Their differences are as follows:

（1）<ruby>用<rt>Yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>宾语<rt>bīnyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>确指<rt>quèzhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>，<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>宾语<rt>bīnyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>确指<rt>quèzhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>，<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>确指<rt>quèzhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。<ruby>因此<rt>Yīncǐ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>“<ruby>书房<rt>Shūfáng</rt></ruby><ruby>旁边<rt>pángbiān</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>卧室<rt>wòshì</rt></ruby>。”<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>“<ruby>书房<rt>Shūfáng</rt></ruby><ruby>旁边<rt>pángbiān</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>卧室<rt>wòshì</rt></ruby>。”  
The object of a sentence with “有 (yǒu)” is indefinite, while the object of a sentence with “是 (shì)” is either definite or indefinite. As a result, “书房旁边有我的卧室 (shūfáng pángbiān yǒu wǒ de wòshì)。” would be incorrect, and “书房旁边是我的卧室 (shūfáng pángbiān shì wǒ de wòshì)。” is correct.

4. ✗ <ruby>书房<rt>Shūfáng</rt></ruby><ruby>旁边<rt>pángbiān</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>卧室<rt>wòshì</rt></ruby>。  
   Wrong — “my bedroom” is a specific thing, so 有 (yǒu) can't introduce it.

5. ✓ <ruby>书房<rt>Shūfáng</rt></ruby><ruby>旁边<rt>pángbiān</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>卧室<rt>wòshì</rt></ruby>。  
   Right — “Next to the study is my bedroom.”

（2）<ruby>用<rt>Yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>只<rt>zhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>说明<rt>shuōmíng</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>处<rt>chù</rt></ruby><ruby>存在<rt>cúnzài</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>物<rt>wù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>已<rt>yǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>知<rt>zhī</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>处<rt>chù</rt></ruby><ruby>存在<rt>cúnzài</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>物<rt>wù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>进<rt>jìn</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>步<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>说明<rt>shuōmíng</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>谁<rt>shéi</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby>。  
A sentence with “有 (yǒu)” just indicates a person or thing is located somewhere, while a sentence with “是 (shì)” indicates it is already known that somebody/something is there, and who/what it is is further stated.

> 💡 Quick test: if you could say “there is a…” use 有 (yǒu) (楼上有三个房间 (lóushàng yǒu sān ge fángjiān) “there are three rooms upstairs”). If you already know something is there and you're saying *what* it is, use 是 (shì) (北边是书房 (běibian shì shūfáng) “the thing to the north is the study”). If the thing comes first, use 在 (zài) (厨房在客厅旁边 (chúfáng zài kètīng pángbiān)).

### 2. “的”字结构 (“de” zì jiégòu) — The “的 (de)” phrase

<ruby>名词<rt>Míngcí</rt></ruby>、<ruby>代词<rt>dàicí</rt></ruby>、<ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby>、<ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>等<rt>děng</rt></ruby><ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby><ruby>加上<rt>jiāshàng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>组成<rt>zǔchéng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>”<ruby>字<rt>zì</rt></ruby><ruby>结构<rt>jiégòu</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>的<rt>De</rt></ruby>”<ruby>字<rt>zì</rt></ruby><ruby>结构<rt>jiégòu</rt></ruby><ruby>相当于<rt>xiāngdāngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>名词<rt>míngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>名词<rt>míngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>短语<rt>duǎnyǔ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
If a noun, pronoun, adjective or verb is followed by “的 (de)”, the “的 (de)” phrase is formed, which is equivalent to a noun or noun phrase, e.g.,

1. <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>套<rt>tào</rt></ruby><ruby>房子<rt>fángzi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shàng</rt></ruby><ruby>下<rt>xià</rt></ruby><ruby>两<rt>liǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>层<rt>céng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   This house is a two-storey one.

2. <ruby>这个<rt>Zhège</rt></ruby><ruby>卧室<rt>wòshì</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>？  
   Is this bedroom yours?

3. <ruby>你<rt>Nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>喜欢<rt>xǐhuan</rt></ruby><ruby>红<rt>hóng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby><ruby>绿<rt>lǜ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>？  
   Do you like the red one or the green one?

4. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>买<rt>mǎi</rt></ruby><ruby>点儿<rt>diǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   I want to buy something to eat.

> 💡 The noun after 的 (de) is dropped because it's obvious from context: 你的 (nǐ de)（卧室 (wòshì)） “yours”, 红的 (hóng de)（苹果 (píngguǒ)） “the red one”, 吃的 (chī de)（东西 (dōngxi)） “something to eat”. Pronoun + 的 (de) = possession (你的 (nǐ de)), adjective + 的 (de) = “the … one” (红的 (hóng de), 大的 (dà de)), verb + 的 (de) = “things for …-ing” (吃的 (chī de), 喝的 (hē de), 用的 (yòng de)).

### 3. 什么（+名词）都…… (shénme (+míngcí) dōu……) — The structure 什么 (shénme)（+noun）都…… (dōu……)

<ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby>“<ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby>（+<ruby>名词<rt>míngcí</rt></ruby>）<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>代词<rt>dàicí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>泛指<rt>fànzhǐ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>所有<rt>suǒyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>、<ruby>全部<rt>quánbù</rt></ruby>。<ruby>若<rt>Ruò</rt></ruby>“<ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby>”<ruby>指代<rt>zhǐdài</rt></ruby><ruby>清楚<rt>qīngchu</rt></ruby>，<ruby>中间<rt>zhōngjiān</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>名词<rt>míngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>省略<rt>shěnglüè</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The pronoun “什么 (shénme)” in the structure “什么 (shénme)（+noun）都…… (dōu……)” indicates a general reference, meaning “every” or “all”. If what “什么 (shénme)” refers to is clear, the noun in the middle of the structure can be omitted, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>名词<rt>míngcí</rt></ruby>） + <ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby>……  
> 什么 (shénme) + (noun) + 都 (dōu) + verb… = every / any (noun)

1. （<ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>套<rt>tào</rt></ruby><ruby>房子<rt>fángzi</rt></ruby>）<ruby>沙发<rt>shāfā</rt></ruby>、<ruby>床<rt>chuáng</rt></ruby>、<ruby>电视<rt>diànshì</rt></ruby>、<ruby>冰箱<rt>bīngxiāng</rt></ruby>、<ruby>洗衣机<rt>xǐyījī</rt></ruby>……<ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby>。  
   (This house has) a sofa, bed, TV, fridge, washing machine… it has everything.

2. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>现在<rt>xiànzài</rt></ruby><ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby><ruby>东西<rt>dōngxi</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby>。  
   I don't want to eat anything right now.

> 💡 Positive verb → “everything / every kind of…” (什么水果都有 (shénme shuǐguǒ dōu yǒu) “they have every kind of fruit”). With 不 (bù) or 没 (méi) → “nothing / not any…” (什么都不想吃 (shénme dōu bù xiǎng chī) “don't want to eat anything”). The noun always sits between 什么 (shénme) and 都 (dōu) — never after the verb: ✗ 都有什么家电 (dōu yǒu shénme jiādiàn) → ✓ 什么家电都有 (shénme jiādiàn dōu yǒu).

### Exercise 2. 这是……的吗？ (Zhè shì…… de ma?) — Guess what's in the bag

*班级活动 (bānjí huódòng) — Class activity*

<ruby>请<rt>Qǐng</rt></ruby><ruby>几位<rt>jǐwèi</rt></ruby><ruby>同学<rt>tóngxué</rt></ruby><ruby>每人<rt>měirén</rt></ruby><ruby>往<rt>wǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>大<rt>dà</rt></ruby><ruby>口袋<rt>kǒudài</rt></ruby><ruby>里<rt>li</rt></ruby><ruby>放<rt>fàng</rt></ruby><ruby>一样<rt>yíyàng</rt></ruby><ruby>东西<rt>dōngxi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>比如<rt>bǐrú</rt></ruby><ruby>笔<rt>bǐ</rt></ruby>、<ruby>书<rt>shū</rt></ruby>、<ruby>本子<rt>běnzi</rt></ruby>、<ruby>可口可乐<rt>Kěkǒukělè</rt></ruby><ruby>等<rt>děng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>一名<rt>Yìmíng</rt></ruby><ruby>同学<rt>tóngxué</rt></ruby><ruby>避开<rt>bìkāi</rt></ruby><ruby>大家<rt>dàjiā</rt></ruby><ruby>视线<rt>shìxiàn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>摸<rt>mō</rt></ruby><ruby>出<rt>chū</rt></ruby><ruby>一种<rt>yìzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>东西<rt>dōngxi</rt></ruby><ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>让<rt>ràng</rt></ruby><ruby>同学们<rt>tóngxuémen</rt></ruby><ruby>猜<rt>cāi</rt></ruby><ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby>、<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>谁<rt>shéi</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。<ruby>同学们<rt>Tóngxuémen</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>轮流<rt>lúnliú</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>……<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>？”<ruby>提问<rt>tíwèn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>这位<rt>zhèwèi</rt></ruby><ruby>同学<rt>tóngxué</rt></ruby><ruby>只能<rt>zhǐnéng</rt></ruby><ruby>回答<rt>huídá</rt></ruby>“<ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>……<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>”<ruby>或者<rt>huòzhě</rt></ruby>“<ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>……<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>先猜<rt>xiāncāi</rt></ruby><ruby>对<rt>duì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>获胜<rt>huòshèng</rt></ruby>。  
Ask some students to put something into a big bag, such as a pen, a book, a notebook, Coca-Cola, etc. Taking one piece out, a student keeps others out of sight and asks others to guess what and whose it is. His classmates are required to use the structure “这是……的吗 (zhè shì……de ma)？” to ask him what he is holding, while he can only reply using “这是……的 (zhè shì……de)” or “这不是……的 (zhè bú shì……de)”. The student who makes the correct guess first, wins.

A: <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>？  
Is it something to eat?

B: <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
It's not something to eat.

A: <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>大<rt>dà</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>？
