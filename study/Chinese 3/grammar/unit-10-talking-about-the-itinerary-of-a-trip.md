## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. “或者”与“还是”的区别 (“huòzhě” yǔ “háishi” de qūbié) — Difference between 或者 (huòzhě) and 还是 (háishi)

“<ruby>或者<rt>Huòzhě</rt></ruby>”<ruby>和<rt>hé</rt></ruby>“<ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>选择<rt>xuǎnzé</rt></ruby><ruby>关系<rt>guānxi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但<rt>dàn</rt></ruby>“<ruby>或者<rt>huòzhě</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用于<rt>yòngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>陈述句<rt>chénshùjù</rt></ruby><ruby>里<rt>li</rt></ruby>，<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby>“<ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>则<rt>zé</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>疑问<rt>yíwèn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>句子<rt>jùzi</rt></ruby><ruby>里<rt>li</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“或者 (huòzhě)” and “还是 (háishi)” both express a choice (“or”), but “或者 (huòzhě)” is used in statements, while “还是 (háishi)” is used in questions, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>陈述句<rt>chénshùjù</rt></ruby>：A <ruby>或者<rt>huòzhě</rt></ruby> B　·　<ruby>疑问句<rt>yíwènjù</rt></ruby>：A <ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby> B？  
> statement: A or B (either is fine) · question: A or B? (which one?)

1. <ruby>今天<rt>Jīntiān</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>或者<rt>huòzhě</rt></ruby><ruby>明天<rt>míngtiān</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>行<rt>xíng</rt></ruby>。  
   Going today or tomorrow is fine.

2. <ruby>你<rt>Nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>喝<rt>hē</rt></ruby><ruby>可乐<rt>kělè</rt></ruby><ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby><ruby>茶<rt>chá</rt></ruby>？  
   Will you have cola or tea?

“<ruby>或者<rt>Huòzhě</rt></ruby>”<ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>等同<rt>děngtóng</rt></ruby><ruby>关系<rt>guānxi</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“或者 (huòzhě)” can also link equivalent things, e.g.,

3. <ruby>您<rt>Nín</rt></ruby><ruby>对<rt>duì</rt></ruby><ruby>哪些<rt>nǎxiē</rt></ruby><ruby>城市<rt>chéngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>或者<rt>huòzhě</rt></ruby><ruby>景点<rt>jǐngdiǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>感<rt>gǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>兴趣<rt>xìngqù</rt></ruby><ruby>呢<rt>ne</rt></ruby>？  
   Which cities or sights are you interested in?

> 💡 ✓ 你坐公共汽车或者坐地铁都可以 (nǐ zuò gōnggòngqìchē huòzhě zuòdìtiě dōu kěyǐ) (statement). ✓ 你喜欢风景优美的景点还是喜欢历史名城 (nǐ xǐhuan fēngjǐngyōuměi de jǐngdiǎn háishi xǐhuan lìshǐ míngchéng)？ (question).

### 2. 假设复句：如果/要是……的话，…… (jiǎshè fùjù: rúguǒ / yàoshi…… dehuà, ……) — Hypothetical compound sentence: 如果 (rúguǒ)/要是……的话 (yàoshi……dehuà)，……

“<ruby>如果<rt>Rúguǒ</rt></ruby>/<ruby>要是<rt>yàoshi</rt></ruby>……<ruby>的话<rt>dehuà</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>假设<rt>jiǎshè</rt></ruby>，<ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>常用<rt>chángyòng</rt></ruby><ruby>副词<rt>fùcí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby>”<ruby>承接<rt>chéngjiē</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shàng</rt></ruby><ruby>文<rt>wén</rt></ruby>，<ruby>得出结论<rt>déchūjiélùn</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>如果<rt>Rúguǒ</rt></ruby>/<ruby>要是<rt>yàoshi</rt></ruby>……<ruby>的话<rt>dehuà</rt></ruby>”<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>“<ruby>如果<rt>rúguǒ</rt></ruby>/<ruby>要是<rt>yàoshi</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby>“……<ruby>的话<rt>dehuà</rt></ruby>”。“<ruby>如果<rt>Rúguǒ</rt></ruby>”<ruby>和<rt>hé</rt></ruby>“<ruby>要是<rt>yàoshi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>基本相同<rt>jīběnxiàngtóng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但<rt>dàn</rt></ruby>“<ruby>如果<rt>rúguǒ</rt></ruby>”<ruby>更<rt>gèng</rt></ruby><ruby>正式<rt>zhèngshì</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>的话<rt>Dehuà</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>假设<rt>jiǎshè</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>后面<rt>hòumian</rt></ruby>，<ruby>引起<rt>yǐnqǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>下文<rt>xiàwén</rt></ruby>，<ruby>一般<rt>yìbān</rt></ruby><ruby>用于<rt>yòngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>口语<rt>kǒuyǔ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“如果 (rúguǒ)/要是……的话 (yàoshi……dehuà)” expresses a hypothesis; the second clause often uses the adverb “就 (jiù)” to draw the conclusion. You can also say just “如果 (rúguǒ)/要是…… (yàoshi……)” or just “……的话 (dehuà)”. “如果 (rúguǒ)” and “要是 (yàoshi)” mean the same, but “如果 (rúguǒ)” is more formal. “的话 (dehuà)” goes at the end of the “if” clause and is mostly spoken, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>如果<rt>rúguǒ</rt></ruby>／<ruby>要是<rt>yàoshi</rt></ruby> + <ruby>条件<rt>tiáojiàn</rt></ruby> + <ruby>的话<rt>dehuà</rt></ruby>，（<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby> + <ruby>结果<rt>jiéguǒ</rt></ruby>  
> if + condition (+ 的话 (dehuà)), (subject) + 就 (jiù) + result

1. <ruby>如果<rt>Rúguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>这样<rt>zhèyàng</rt></ruby><ruby>的话<rt>dehuà</rt></ruby>，<ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>认为<rt>rènwéi</rt></ruby><ruby>我们<rt>wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>新<rt>xīn</rt></ruby><ruby>开<rt>kāi</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>条<rt>tiáo</rt></ruby><ruby>历史<rt>lìshǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>名城<rt>míngchéng</rt></ruby><ruby>线路<rt>xiànlù</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>适合<rt>shìhé</rt></ruby><ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby>。  
   In that case, I think our new historic-cities route would suit you well.

2. <ruby>如果<rt>Rúguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>坐<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>出租车<rt>chūzūchē</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>大概<rt>dàgài</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>děi</rt></ruby><ruby>两<rt>liǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>小时<rt>xiǎoshí</rt></ruby>。  
   If you go by taxi, it'll take about two hours.

3. <ruby>要是<rt>Yàoshi</rt></ruby><ruby>明天<rt>míngtiān</rt></ruby><ruby>天气<rt>tiānqì</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>的话<rt>dehuà</rt></ruby>，<ruby>我们<rt>wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>爬<rt>pá</rt></ruby><ruby>山<rt>shān</rt></ruby>。  
   If the weather's good tomorrow, we'll go hiking.

4. <ruby>要是<rt>Yàoshi</rt></ruby><ruby>买<rt>mǎi</rt></ruby><ruby>生活<rt>shēnghuó</rt></ruby><ruby>用品<rt>yòngpǐn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>一般<rt>yìbān</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>超市<rt>chāoshì</rt></ruby>。  
   If I'm buying household things, I usually go to the supermarket.

5. <ruby>买<rt>Mǎi</rt></ruby><ruby>水果<rt>shuǐguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>蔬菜<rt>shūcài</rt></ruby><ruby>的话<rt>dehuà</rt></ruby>，<ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>最好<rt>zuìhǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>市场<rt>shìchǎng</rt></ruby>。  
   If you're buying fruit and veg, you'd better go to the market.

> 💡 的话 (dehuà) sits at the end of the “if” clause, before the comma: ✓ 如果你想游览历史名城的话 (rúguǒ nǐ xiǎng yóulǎn lìshǐ míngchéng dehuà)，你可以去西安 (nǐ kěyǐ qù Xī'ān) — ✗ …，的话你可以去西安 (dehuà nǐ kěyǐ qù Xī'ān). Don't double up (✗ 要是我有时间如果 (yàoshi wǒ yǒu shíjiān rúguǒ)).

### 3. 对……感兴趣 (duì…… gǎn xìngqù) — Interested in …

> 💡 Tested in the self-test (result B): the negative of 对……感兴趣 (duì……gǎnxìngqù) is 对……不感兴趣 (duì……bùgǎnxìngqù) — 不 (bù) goes right before 感兴趣 (gǎnxìngqù), not before 对 (duì).

1. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>对<rt>duì</rt></ruby><ruby>那个<rt>nàge</rt></ruby><ruby>景点<rt>jǐngdiǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>感<rt>gǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>兴趣<rt>xìngqù</rt></ruby>。  
   I'm not interested in that sight. (✗ 我不对那个景点感兴趣 (wǒ bú duì nàge jǐngdiǎn gǎnxìngqù))
