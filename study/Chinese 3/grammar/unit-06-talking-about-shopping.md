# Chinese 3 · Unit 6: 谈购物 (Tán gòuwù) — Talking about Shopping

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 99, 106–107, 111–112, 114–115 (printed pp. 83, 90–91, 95–96, 98–99)

This unit is about where and how to shop. Grammar: (1) 虽然…… (suīrán……)，但是…… (dànshì……) “although…, (but)…” and how 不过 (búguò) differs from 但是 (dànshì); (2) A 不如 (bùrú) B “A is not as good as B”; (3) making suggestions with ……，好吗 (hǎo ma)？ / 怎么样 (zěnmeyàng)？ / 行吗 (xíng ma)？. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 重点语句 (Zhòngdiǎn yǔjù) — Key sentences

1. <ruby>买<rt>Mǎi</rt></ruby><ruby>衣服<rt>yīfu</rt></ruby>、<ruby>鞋<rt>xié</rt></ruby><ruby>什么的<rt>shénmede</rt></ruby>，<ruby>应该<rt>yīnggāi</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>哪儿<rt>nǎr</rt></ruby><ruby>啊<rt>a</rt></ruby>？  
   Where should you go to buy clothes, shoes and things like that?

2. （<ruby>去<rt>Qù</rt></ruby><ruby>服装<rt>fúzhuāng</rt></ruby><ruby>市场<rt>shìchǎng</rt></ruby>）<ruby>虽然<rt>suīrán</rt></ruby><ruby>东西<rt>dōngxi</rt></ruby><ruby>便宜<rt>piányi</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby><ruby>质量<rt>zhìliàng</rt></ruby><ruby>可能<rt>kěnéng</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby><ruby>商场<rt>shāngchǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby>。  
   (At a clothing market) although things are much cheaper, the quality may not be as good as in a department store.

3. <ruby>星期天<rt>Xīngqītiān</rt></ruby><ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>陪<rt>péi</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>逛<rt>guàng</rt></ruby><ruby>雅秀<rt>Yǎxiù</rt></ruby><ruby>市场<rt>shìchǎng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>怎么样<rt>zěnmeyàng</rt></ruby>？  
   How about you come with me to Yaxiu Market on Sunday?

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 转折复句：虽然……，但是…… (zhuǎnzhé fùjù: suīrán……, dànshì……) — Transitional compound sentence: 虽然…… (suīrán……)，但是…… (dànshì……)

“<ruby>虽然<rt>Suīrán</rt></ruby>……<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>构成<rt>gòuchéng</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>转折<rt>zhuǎnzhé</rt></ruby><ruby>关系<rt>guānxi</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>复句<rt>fùjù</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>虽然<rt>Suīrán</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用于<rt>yòngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>第一<rt>dì-yī</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>既<rt>jì</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>前<rt>qián</rt></ruby>，<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>但是<rt>Dànshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>应该<rt>yīnggāi</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>第二<rt>dì-èr</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>句首<rt>jùshǒu</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“虽然……但是…… (suīrán……dànshì……)” forms a transitional compound sentence. “虽然 (suīrán)” is used in the first clause, either before or after the subject, and “但是 (dànshì)” must be at the beginning of the second clause, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby>）<ruby>虽然<rt>suīrán</rt></ruby>……，<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby> + <ruby>第二<rt>dì-èr</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby>  
> (subject) although…, but + second clause — 但是 (dànshì) always starts the second clause

1. <ruby>虽然<rt>Suīrán</rt></ruby><ruby>我们<rt>wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>累<rt>lèi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby><ruby>我们<rt>wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>高兴<rt>gāoxìng</rt></ruby>。  
   Although we're tired, we're all very happy.

“<ruby>不过<rt>Búguò</rt></ruby>”<ruby>和<rt>hé</rt></ruby>“<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>转折<rt>zhuǎnzhé</rt></ruby><ruby>语气<rt>yǔqì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>连词<rt>liáncí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>半句<rt>bànjù</rt></ruby>。<ruby>二者<rt>Èrzhě</rt></ruby><ruby>不同之处<rt>bùtóngzhīchù</rt></ruby><ruby>在于<rt>zàiyú</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>不过<rt>búguò</rt></ruby>”<ruby>只<rt>zhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>轻微<rt>qīngwēi</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>转折<rt>zhuǎnzhé</rt></ruby>，<ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby>“<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>转折<rt>zhuǎnzhé</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>语气<rt>yǔqì</rt></ruby><ruby>强烈<rt>qiángliè</rt></ruby>；“<ruby>不过<rt>búguò</rt></ruby>”<ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>用于<rt>yòngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>口语<rt>kǒuyǔ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“不过 (búguò)” and “但是 (dànshì)” are both conjunctions used in the second clause to indicate a turn. The difference: “不过 (búguò)” shows only a slight turn and is weaker than “但是 (dànshì)”; “不过 (búguò)” is mostly used in spoken Chinese, e.g.,

2. <ruby>那个<rt>Nàge</rt></ruby><ruby>手机<rt>shǒujī</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>漂亮<rt>piàoliang</rt></ruby>，<ruby>不过<rt>búguò</rt></ruby><ruby>有点儿<rt>yǒudiǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>贵<rt>guì</rt></ruby>。  
   That phone is very nice, though it's a bit expensive.

3. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>时间<rt>shíjiān</rt></ruby>。  
   I'd really like to go, but I don't have time.

> 💡 ✓ 虽然这个电视比较贵 (suīrán zhège diànshì bǐjiào guì)，但是质量非常好 (dànshì zhìliàng fēicháng hǎo). ✗ …，质量但是非常好 (zhìliàng dànshì fēicháng hǎo) — 但是 (dànshì) can't come after the subject of the second clause.

### 2. “不如”表示比较 (“bùrú” biǎoshì bǐjiào) — 不如 (bùrú) indicating comparison

“A<ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby>B”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>“A<ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby>B<ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby>”。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“A不如 (bùrú)B” means “A is not as good as B”, e.g.,

1. <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>本<rt>běn</rt></ruby><ruby>书<rt>shū</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby><ruby>那<rt>nà</rt></ruby><ruby>本<rt>běn</rt></ruby><ruby>书<rt>shū</rt></ruby>。  
   This book isn't as good as that one.

“<ruby>不如<rt>Bùrú</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby><ruby>一般<rt>yìbān</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>积极意义<rt>jījíyìyì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>词语<rt>cíyǔ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“不如 (bùrú)” is generally followed by words with a positive meaning, e.g.,

2. <ruby>他<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby><ruby>哥哥<rt>gēge</rt></ruby><ruby>高<rt>gāo</rt></ruby>。  
   He isn't as tall as his elder brother.

“A<ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby>B+<ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby>”<ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>前<rt>qián</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>副词<rt>fùcí</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
In “A不如 (bùrú)B + adjective”, the adjective is not preceded by an adverb, e.g.,

3. <ruby>这个<rt>Zhège</rt></ruby><ruby>手机<rt>shǒujī</rt></ruby><ruby>质量<rt>zhìliàng</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby><ruby>那个<rt>nàge</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby>。  
   The quality of this phone isn't as good as that one's.

> **Pattern:** A + <ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby> + B + （<ruby>积极<rt>jījí</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby>）  
> A is not as (good/big/many…) as B — positive adjective, no 很 (hěn)

> 💡 ✓ 商店的东西不如市场的多 (shāngdiàn de dōngxi bùrú shìchǎng de duō) (not as many) — ✗ 不如市场的少 (bùrú shìchǎng de shǎo). ✓ 不如水果好吃 (bùrú shuǐguǒ hǎochī) — ✗ 不如水果很好吃 (bùrú shuǐguǒ hěn hǎochī).

### 3. 用“好吗”“怎么样”“行吗”的疑问句 (yòng “hǎo ma” “zěnmeyàng” “xíng ma” de yíwènjù) — Questions with 好吗 (hǎo ma), 怎么样 (zěnmeyàng) and 行吗 (xíng ma)

<ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby><ruby>提出<rt>tíchū</rt></ruby><ruby>建议<rt>jiànyì</rt></ruby><ruby>征询<rt>zhēngxún</rt></ruby><ruby>对方<rt>duìfāng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意见<rt>yìjiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>时<rt>shí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>使用<rt>shǐyòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>”“<ruby>怎么样<rt>zěnmeyàng</rt></ruby>”“<ruby>行<rt>xíng</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>”<ruby>提问<rt>tíwèn</rt></ruby>。<ruby>问句<rt>Wènjù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>前<rt>qián</rt></ruby><ruby>一部分<rt>yíbùfen</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>陈述句<rt>chénshùjù</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
When making a suggestion and asking for the other person's opinion, add “好吗 (hǎo ma)”, “怎么样 (zěnmeyàng)” or “行吗 (xíng ma)” after a statement, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>陈述句<rt>chénshùjù</rt></ruby>（<ruby>建议<rt>jiànyì</rt></ruby>），+ <ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>？／<ruby>怎么样<rt>zěnmeyàng</rt></ruby>？／<ruby>行<rt>xíng</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>？  
> suggestion, + OK? / how about it? / is that alright?

1. <ruby>我们<rt>Wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>喝<rt>hē</rt></ruby><ruby>茶<rt>chá</rt></ruby>，<ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>？  
   Let's have some tea, OK?

2. <ruby>星期天<rt>Xīngqītiān</rt></ruby><ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>陪<rt>péi</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>逛<rt>guàng</rt></ruby><ruby>雅秀<rt>Yǎxiù</rt></ruby><ruby>市场<rt>shìchǎng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>怎么样<rt>zěnmeyàng</rt></ruby>？  
   How about you come with me to Yaxiu Market on Sunday?

3. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>明天<rt>míngtiān</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>行<rt>xíng</rt></ruby><ruby>吗<rt>ma</rt></ruby>？  
   Is it alright if I go tomorrow?

<ruby>表示同意<rt>Biǎoshìtóngyì</rt></ruby><ruby>时<rt>shí</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby>”“<ruby>行<rt>xíng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>回答<rt>huídá</rt></ruby>。  
Reply with “好 (hǎo)” or “行 (xíng)” to show agreement.

> 💡 These ask for agreement, so answer with agreement or refusal (好啊 (hǎo a) / 行 (xíng) / 不行 (bùxíng)，我… (wǒ…)), not with a description: ✗ 雅秀市场很好 (yǎ xiù shìchǎng hěn hǎo).

Book's scoring: all right — 太棒了 (tàibàngle)！. Missed Q5 — “……，怎么样 (zěnmeyàng)？” asks whether you accept a suggestion (note 3). Missed Q1 or Q3 — review 不如 (bùrú) (note 2). Missed Q2 or Q4 — review 虽然……但是…… (suīrán……dànshì……) (note 1).

**(1)** <ruby>商店<rt>Shāngdiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>东西<rt>dōngxi</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby><ruby>市场<rt>shìchǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby>。  
**A** — 不如 (bùrú) is normally followed by a positive-meaning word (多 (duō), 好 (hǎo), 大… (dà…)). (grammar note 2)

**(2)** <ruby>虽然<rt>Suīrán</rt></ruby><ruby>这个<rt>zhège</rt></ruby><ruby>电视<rt>diànshì</rt></ruby><ruby>比较<rt>bǐjiào</rt></ruby><ruby>贵<rt>guì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby><ruby>质量<rt>zhìliàng</rt></ruby><ruby>非常<rt>fēicháng</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby>。  
**A** — 但是 (dànshì) goes at the very start of the second clause. (grammar note 1)

**(3)** <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>觉得<rt>juéde</rt></ruby><ruby>蔬菜<rt>shūcài</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby><ruby>水果<rt>shuǐguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>好吃<rt>hǎochī</rt></ruby>。  
**A** — No adverb (很 (hěn)) before the adjective in A 不如 (bùrú) B + adjective. (grammar note 2)

**(4)** <ruby>那个<rt>Nàge</rt></ruby><ruby>商场<rt>shāngchǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>虽然<rt>suīrán</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>大<rt>dà</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby><ruby>东西<rt>dōngxi</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby>。  
**A** — 虽然 (suīrán) may follow the subject, but 但是 (dànshì) must start the second clause. (grammar note 1)

**(5)** <ruby>好<rt>Hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>啊<rt>a</rt></ruby>。  
**B** — “……，怎么样 (zěnmeyàng)？” asks for your opinion of a suggestion — answer by agreeing (好啊 (hǎo a)) or declining. (grammar note 3)
