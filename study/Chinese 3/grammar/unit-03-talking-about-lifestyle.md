# Chinese 3 · Unit 3: 谈生活习惯 (Tán shēnghuóxíguàn) — Talking about Lifestyle

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 49, 54–55, 57–60 (printed pp. 33, 38–39, 41–44)

This unit is about daily routines and habits. Grammar: (1) 因为…… (yīnwèi……)，所以…… (suǒyǐ……) “because…, so…”; (2) 为了…… (wèile……) “in order to…”; (3) the three structural particles 地 (de), 得 (de) and 的 (de) (all pronounced de). Useful phrases: 对……来说 (duì……láishuō), 拿……来说 (ná……láishuō), 说实话 (shuōshíhuà). Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 因果复句：因为……，所以…… (yīnguǒ fùjù: yīnwèi……, suǒyǐ……) — Cause-effect compound sentence: 因为…… (yīnwèi……)，所以…… (suǒyǐ……)

“<ruby>因为<rt>Yīnwèi</rt></ruby>……，<ruby>所以<rt>suǒyǐ</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>两<rt>liǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>因果<rt>yīnguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>关系<rt>guānxì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby>。<ruby>前<rt>Qián</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>原因<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>结果<rt>jiéguǒ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“因为…… (yīnwèi……)，所以…… (suǒyǐ……)” connects two clauses indicating cause and effect, with the first one indicating the cause and the second one indicating the effect, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>因为<rt>yīnwèi</rt></ruby> + <ruby>原因<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>所以<rt>suǒyǐ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>结果<rt>jiéguǒ</rt></ruby>  
> because + cause, so + result

1. <ruby>因为<rt>Yīnwèi</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>生<rt>shēng</rt></ruby><ruby>病<rt>bìng</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>所以<rt>suǒyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>今天<rt>jīntiān</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shàng</rt></ruby><ruby>课<rt>kè</rt></ruby>。  
   Because he's ill, he didn't go to class today.

2. <ruby>因为<rt>Yīnwèi</rt></ruby><ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>几<rt>jǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>天<rt>tiān</rt></ruby><ruby>一直<rt>yìzhí</rt></ruby><ruby>下<rt>xià</rt></ruby><ruby>雨<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>所以<rt>suǒyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>我们<rt>wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>不得不<rt>bùdébù</rt></ruby><ruby>推迟了<rt>tuīchíle</rt></ruby><ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>次<rt>cì</rt></ruby><ruby>行程<rt>xíngchéng</rt></ruby>。  
   Because it's been raining for days, we had to postpone this trip.

> 💡 Cause first, result second: ✓ 因为我发烧了 (yīnwèi wǒ fāshāo le)，所以没来上课 (suǒyǐ méi lái shàngkè). Reversing them (✗ 因为没来上课 (yīnwèi méi lái shàngkè)，所以发烧了 (suǒyǐ fāshāo le)) says the absence caused the fever. English uses just one of “because/so”; Chinese happily uses both.

### 2. 目的复句：为了…… (mùdì fùjù: wèile……) — Compound sentence of purpose: 为了…… (wèile……)

“<ruby>为了<rt>Wèile</rt></ruby>……，……”<ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>“<ruby>为了<rt>wèile</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>目的<rt>mùdì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>第一<rt>dì-yī</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>第二<rt>dì-èr</rt></ruby><ruby>分句<rt>fēnjù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>即<rt>jí</rt></ruby>“……，<ruby>为了<rt>wèile</rt></ruby>……”。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
In “为了…… (wèile……)，……”, “为了…… (wèile……)” indicates the purpose and can be used either in the first clause or in the second clause, i.e. “……，为了…… (wèile……)”, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>为了<rt>wèile</rt></ruby> + <ruby>目的<rt>mùdì</rt></ruby>，+ <ruby>做法<rt>zuòfǎ</rt></ruby> ／ <ruby>做法<rt>zuòfǎ</rt></ruby>，+ <ruby>为了<rt>wèile</rt></ruby> + <ruby>目的<rt>mùdì</rt></ruby>  
> in order to + goal, + action / action, + in order to + goal

1. <ruby>为了<rt>Wèile</rt></ruby><ruby>学好<rt>xuéhǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>汉语<rt>Hànyǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>大龙<rt>Dàlóng</rt></ruby><ruby>来到<rt>láidào</rt></ruby><ruby>中国<rt>Zhōngguó</rt></ruby>。  
   In order to learn Chinese well, Dalong came to China.

2. <ruby>大龙<rt>Dàlóng</rt></ruby><ruby>来到<rt>láidào</rt></ruby><ruby>中国<rt>Zhōngguó</rt></ruby>，<ruby>为了<rt>wèile</rt></ruby><ruby>学好<rt>xuéhǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>汉语<rt>Hànyǔ</rt></ruby>。  
   Dalong came to China in order to learn Chinese well.

<ruby>注意<rt>Zhùyì</rt></ruby>：“<ruby>为了<rt>wèile</rt></ruby>”<ruby>与<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby>“<ruby>因为<rt>yīnwèi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>不同<rt>bùtóng</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>因为<rt>yīnwèi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>原因<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>前提<rt>qiántí</rt></ruby><ruby>条件<rt>tiáojiàn</rt></ruby>；<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby>“<ruby>为了<rt>wèile</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>目的<rt>mùdì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>前提<rt>qiántí</rt></ruby><ruby>条件<rt>tiáojiàn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>得到<rt>dédào</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>结果<rt>jiéguǒ</rt></ruby>。  
Note: “为了 (wèile)” is not equivalent to “因为 (yīnwèi)”. “因为 (yīnwèi)” indicates the reason, showing the prerequisite; while “为了 (wèile)” indicates the purpose — the result you want to get, not the prerequisite.

> 💡 为了 (wèile) goes in front of the whole goal: ✓ 为了学好汉语 (wèile xuéhǎo Hànyǔ), ✗ 学好为了汉语 (xuéhǎo wèile Hànyǔ). The goal is what you do the action *for*: 为了早上能按时到校 (wèile zǎoshang néng ànshí dàoxiào)，我晚上 (wǒ wǎnshang)11点就睡 (diǎn jiù shuì) (sleep early → to be on time), not the other way round.

### 3. “地”“得”“的”的区别 (“de” “de” “de” de qūbié) — Difference between 地 (de), 得 (de) and 的 (de)

“<ruby>地<rt>De</rt></ruby>”“<ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby>”“<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>”<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>结构<rt>jiégòu</rt></ruby><ruby>助词<rt>zhùcí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但<rt>dàn</rt></ruby><ruby>用法<rt>yòngfǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>不同<rt>bùtóng</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>地<rt>De</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>状语<rt>zhuàngyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby>、<ruby>补语<rt>bǔyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>前边<rt>qiánbian</rt></ruby>，<ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby>“<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>”<ruby>则<rt>zé</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>定语<rt>dìngyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“地 (de)”, “得 (de)” and “的 (de)” are structural particles with different usages. “地 (de)” is used after an adverbial, “得 (de)” is used after a verb or an adjective and before a complement, while “的 (de)” is used after an attributive, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>状语<rt>zhuàngyǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>地<rt>de</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby>　·　<ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby> + <ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby> + <ruby>补语<rt>bǔyǔ</rt></ruby>　·　<ruby>定语<rt>dìngyǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby> + <ruby>名词<rt>míngcí</rt></ruby>  
> adverbial + 地 (de) + verb (how you do it) · verb + 得 (de) + complement (how well/what result) · modifier + 的 (de) + noun (which/whose)

1. <ruby>慢慢<rt>Mànmàn</rt></ruby><ruby>地<rt>de</rt></ruby>，<ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>习惯了<rt>xíguànle</rt></ruby><ruby>早<rt>zǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>睡<rt>shuì</rt></ruby><ruby>早<rt>zǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>起<rt>qǐ</rt></ruby>。  
   Gradually, I got used to early to bed and early to rise. (地 (de) after an adverbial)

2. <ruby>她<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>高兴<rt>gāoxìng</rt></ruby><ruby>地<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>：“<ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>真<rt>zhēn</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>太<rt>tài</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>！”  
   She said happily: “That's really great!” (地 (de))

3. <ruby>她<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>每<rt>měi</rt></ruby><ruby>天<rt>tiān</rt></ruby><ruby>睡<rt>shuì</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>晚<rt>wǎn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>起<rt>qǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>早<rt>zǎo</rt></ruby>。  
   She goes to bed very late and gets up very early every day. (得 (de) before a complement)

4. <ruby>那<rt>Nà</rt></ruby><ruby>时候<rt>shíhou</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>典型<rt>diǎnxíng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>夜猫子<rt>yèmāozi</rt></ruby>。  
   Back then I was a typical night owl. (的 (de) after an attributive)

### 4. 常用说法 (chángyòng shuōfǎ) — Useful phrases in this unit

> 💡 Not numbered grammar notes in the book, but key sentences of the unit:

1. <ruby>对<rt>Duì</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>来说<rt>láishuō</rt></ruby>，<ruby>早起<rt>zǎoqǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>太<rt>tài</rt></ruby><ruby>难<rt>nán</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。

   说实话 (shuōshíhuà) = “to tell the truth”: To be honest, I don't really like getting up early.
