# Chinese 4 · Unit 5: 谈压力 (Tán yālì) — Talking about Pressure

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 81, 87–90, 90–93 (printed pp. 65, 71–77)

This unit is about stress and giving advice. Grammar: (1) 省得 (shěngde) “so as to avoid…”; (2) adjective + 得不得了 (de bùdéliǎo) “extremely…”; (3) 不妨 (bùfáng) “you might as well…”. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 重点语句 (Zhòngdiǎn yǔjù) — Key sentences

1. <ruby>把<rt>Bǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>该<rt>gāi</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>逐一<rt>zhúyī</rt></ruby><ruby>记<rt>jì</rt></ruby><ruby>下来<rt>xiàlai</rt></ruby>，<ruby>省得<rt>shěngde</rt></ruby><ruby>忘<rt>wàng</rt></ruby>。

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 省得 (shěngde) — The usage of 省得 (shěngde)

<ruby>连词<rt>Liáncí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>省得<rt>shěngde</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>一句<rt>yíjù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>开头<rt>kāitóu</rt></ruby>，<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>避免<rt>bìmiǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>希望<rt>xīwàng</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The conjunction “省得 (shěngde)” begins the second clause and means “so as to avoid” something you don't want to happen, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事<rt>shì</rt></ruby>），<ruby>省得<rt>shěngde</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>希望<rt>xīwàng</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事<rt>shì</rt></ruby>）  
> (do this), so that (the unwanted thing) doesn't happen

1. <ruby>多<rt>Duō</rt></ruby><ruby>穿<rt>chuān</rt></ruby><ruby>点儿<rt>diǎnr</rt></ruby>，<ruby>省得<rt>shěngde</rt></ruby><ruby>感冒<rt>gǎnmào</rt></ruby>。  
   Wear more so you don't catch a cold.

2. <ruby>到了<rt>Dàole</rt></ruby><ruby>学校<rt>xuéxiào</rt></ruby><ruby>打<rt>dǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>电话<rt>diànhuà</rt></ruby>，<ruby>省得<rt>shěngde</rt></ruby><ruby>家人<rt>jiārén</rt></ruby><ruby>担心<rt>dānxīn</rt></ruby>。  
   Call when you get to school so your family won't worry.

> 💡 省得 (shěngde) goes in front of the thing to avoid, in the second clause: ✓ 带把伞吧 (dài bǎ sǎn ba)，省得一会儿下雨被淋到 (shěngde yíhuìr xiàyǔ bèi lín dào). ✗ 省得带把伞 (shěngde dài bǎ sǎn)，… (that would mean “to avoid taking an umbrella”).

### 2. 形容词＋得不得了 (xíngróngcí + de bùdéliǎo) — Adjective + 得不得了 (de bùdéliǎo)

“<ruby>得<rt>De</rt></ruby><ruby>不得了<rt>bùdéliǎo</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>补语<rt>bǔyǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>程度<rt>chéngdù</rt></ruby><ruby>高<rt>gāo</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“得不得了 (de bùdéliǎo)” follows an adjective as a complement, meaning “extremely”, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby> + <ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>不得了<rt>bùdéliǎo</rt></ruby>  
> subject + adjective + 得不得了 (de bùdéliǎo) = terribly / extremely + adjective

1. <ruby>他<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>跑<rt>pǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>步<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>速度<rt>sùdù</rt></ruby><ruby>真<rt>zhēn</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>快<rt>kuài</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>不得了<rt>bùdéliǎo</rt></ruby>。  
   He runs incredibly fast.

2. <ruby>最近<rt>Zuìjìn</rt></ruby><ruby>北京<rt>Běijīng</rt></ruby><ruby>热<rt>rè</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>不得了<rt>bùdéliǎo</rt></ruby>。  
   Beijing has been unbearably hot lately.

> 💡 The adjective comes right before 得不得了 (de bùdéliǎo) and after the subject: ✓ 这次考试难得不得了 (zhècì kǎoshì nándé bùdéliǎo). ✗ 这次难考试得不得了 (zhècì nán kǎoshì de bùdéliǎo).

### 3. 不妨 (bùfáng) — The usage of 不妨 (bùfáng)

<ruby>副词<rt>Fùcí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>不妨<rt>bùfáng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>这样<rt>zhèyàng</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby>，<ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby><ruby>产生<rt>chǎnshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>消极影响<rt>xiāojíyǐngxiǎng</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>不妨<rt>Bùfáng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>用于<rt>yòngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>建议<rt>jiànyì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>常有<rt>chángyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>推荐<rt>tuījiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>尝试<rt>chángshì</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The adverb “不妨 (bùfáng)” means there's no harm in doing something. It's mostly used for suggestions, often recommending you give something a try, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>不妨<rt>bùfáng</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby>（<ruby>试试<rt>shìshi</rt></ruby>／<ruby>看看<rt>kànkan</rt></ruby>……）  
> (you) + might as well / why not + verb

1. <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>本<rt>běn</rt></ruby><ruby>书<rt>shū</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby>，<ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>空<rt>kòng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>时候<rt>shíhou</rt></ruby><ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>不妨<rt>bùfáng</rt></ruby><ruby>看看<rt>kànkan</rt></ruby>。  
   This book is great — have a look when you're free.

2. <ruby>你<rt>Nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>如果<rt>rúguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>同意<rt>tóngyì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>不妨<rt>bùfáng</rt></ruby><ruby>直<rt>zhí</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>。  
   If you disagree, you may as well just say so.

> 💡 不妨 (bùfáng) goes right before the suggested action: ✓ 您不妨试试 (nín bùfáng shìshi) / 你不妨看看 (nǐ bùfáng kànkan). ✗ 这个电影不妨很好看 (zhège diànyǐng bùfáng hěn hǎokàn) / ✗ 不妨有好处 (bùfáng yǒu hǎochù).
