**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 157, 164–166, 171–174 (printed pp. 143, 150–152, 157–160)

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. “其中”和“在……中” (“qízhōng” hé “zài…… zhōng”) — The difference between 其中 (qízhōng) and 在……中 (zài……zhōng)

“<ruby>其中<rt>Qízhōng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>指定<rt>zhǐdìng</rt></ruby><ruby>内容<rt>nèiróng</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>部分<rt>bùfen</rt></ruby>。“<ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby>……<ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>引出<rt>yǐnchū</rt></ruby><ruby>指定<rt>zhǐdìng</rt></ruby><ruby>范围<rt>fànwéi</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“其中 (qízhōng)” means “among them / of which” — part of something already mentioned. “在……中 (zài……zhōng)” sets up the range itself (“among …”), e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>整体<rt>zhěngtǐ</rt></ruby>），<ruby>其中<rt>qízhōng</rt></ruby> + <ruby>部分<rt>bùfen</rt></ruby>　·　<ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby> + <ruby>整体<rt>zhěngtǐ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby>，+ <ruby>部分<rt>bùfen</rt></ruby>  
> (the whole is mentioned first), among them + part · among + (the whole), + part

1. <ruby>我们<rt>Wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>班<rt>bān</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>18<rt>shí bā</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>学生<rt>xuésheng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>其中<rt>qízhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>男生<rt>nánshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>10<rt>shí</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>，<ruby>女生<rt>nǚshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>8<rt>bā</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>。  
   Our class has 18 students, of whom 10 are boys and 8 are girls.

2. <ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby><ruby>我们<rt>wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>班<rt>bān</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>18<rt>shí bā</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>学生<rt>xuésheng</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>男生<rt>nánshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>10<rt>shí</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>。  
   Among the 18 students in our class, 10 are boys.

> 💡 其中 (qízhōng) refers back, so the group must already have been mentioned: ✓ 唐代有很多著名的历史人物 (Tángdài yǒu hěnduō zhùmíng de lìshǐ rénwù)，其中… (qízhōng…); ✗ 其中唐代的很多著名的历史人物… (qízhōng Tángdài de hěnduō zhùmíng de lìshǐ rénwù…) To introduce the group yourself, use 在……中 (zài……zhōng): ✓ 在中国的四大发明中 (zài Zhōngguó de sìdàfāmíng zhōng)，…

### 2. 在……看来 (zài…… kànlái) — The usage of 在……看来 (zài……kànlái)

“<ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby>……<ruby>看来<rt>kànlái</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>从<rt>cóng</rt></ruby><ruby>某人<rt>mǒurén</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>角度<rt>jiǎodù</rt></ruby><ruby>考虑<rt>kǎolǜ</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>怎样<rt>zěnyàng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>看法<rt>kànfǎ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“在……看来 (zài……kànlái)” gives someone's opinion from their own point of view, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby> + <ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby> + <ruby>看来<rt>kànlái</rt></ruby>，+ <ruby>看法<rt>kànfǎ</rt></ruby>  
> in (someone)'s view, + opinion

1. <ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>看来<rt>kànlái</rt></ruby>，<ruby>唐朝<rt>Tángcháo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>最<rt>zuì</rt></ruby><ruby>大<rt>dà</rt></ruby><ruby>特点<rt>tèdiǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>开放<rt>kāifàng</rt></ruby>。  
   In my view, the Tang's greatest characteristic was openness.

2. <ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby><ruby>看来<rt>kànlái</rt></ruby>，<ruby>中国<rt>Zhōngguó</rt></ruby><ruby>历史<rt>lìshǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>非常<rt>fēicháng</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby>。  
   To many people, Chinese history is fascinating.

> 💡 在 (zài) comes first and is needed: ✓ 在我看来 (zàiwǒkànlái) — ✗ 我在看来 (wǒ zài kànlái), ✗ 我看来 (wǒ kànlái).

### 3. 究竟 (jiūjìng) — The usage of 究竟 (jiūjìng)

<ruby>副词<rt>Fùcí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>究竟<rt>jiūjìng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>问句<rt>wènjù</rt></ruby><ruby>里<rt>li</rt></ruby>，<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>追究<rt>zhuījiū</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The adverb “究竟 (jiūjìng)” is used in questions to press for the real answer (“exactly / on earth”), e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>究竟<rt>jiūjìng</rt></ruby> + <ruby>疑问<rt>yíwèn</rt></ruby>（<ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby>／<ruby>谁<rt>shéi</rt></ruby>／<ruby>为<rt>wèi</rt></ruby><ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby>／……<ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby>……）  
> subject + 究竟 (jiūjìng) + question part

1. <ruby>你<rt>Nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>一会儿<rt>yíhuìr</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>一会儿<rt>yíhuìr</rt></ruby><ruby>又<rt>yòu</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>究竟<rt>jiūjìng</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby>？  
   One minute you want to go, the next you don't — are you going or not?

2. <ruby>你<rt>Nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>究竟<rt>jiūjìng</rt></ruby><ruby>为<rt>wèi</rt></ruby><ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>高兴<rt>gāoxìng</rt></ruby>？  
   Why on earth are you unhappy?

> 💡 究竟 (jiūjìng) goes before the question part, never at the end: ✓ 你报告的题目究竟是什么 (nǐ bàogào de tímù jiūjìng shì shénme)？ ✗ …是什么究竟 (shì shénme jiūjìng)？
