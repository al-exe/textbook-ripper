# Chinese 4 · Unit 1: 谈休闲娱乐 (Tán xiūxián yúlè) — Talking about Leisure and Entertainment

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 18, 25–26, 28–31 (printed pp. 1, 8–9, 11–14)

This unit is about leisure activities — Beijing opera, Chinese chess, square dancing, theme parks. Grammar: (1) 别说…… (biéshuō……)，就是……也…… (jiùshì……yě……) “let alone…, even… (still)…”; (2) the three uses of 差点儿 (chàdiǎnr) “almost”; (3) 我说呢 (wǒ shuō ne)！ “No wonder!”. ## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 别说……，就是……也…… (biéshuō……, jiùshì…… yě……) — The usage of 别说…… (biéshuō……)，就是……也…… (jiùshì……yě……)

<ruby>这个<rt>Zhège</rt></ruby><ruby>结构<rt>jiégòu</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>别说<rt>biéshuō</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>“<ruby>何况<rt>hékuàng</rt></ruby>、<ruby>不用说<rt>búyòngshuō</rt></ruby>”，“<ruby>就是<rt>jiùshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>假设<rt>jiǎshè</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>让步<rt>ràngbù</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>”<ruby>引出<rt>yǐnchū</rt></ruby><ruby>结论<rt>jiélùn</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>评论<rt>pínglùn</rt></ruby>。<ruby>一般<rt>Yìbān</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>就是<rt>jiùshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后面<rt>hòumian</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>例子<rt>lìzi</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby><ruby>强调<rt>qiángdiào</rt></ruby><ruby>说明<rt>shuōmíng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>别说<rt>biéshuō</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后面<rt>hòumian</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>例子<rt>lìzi</rt></ruby>。<ruby>从<rt>Cóng</rt></ruby><ruby>逻辑<rt>luóji</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shàng</rt></ruby><ruby>来说<rt>láishuō</rt></ruby>，<ruby>如果<rt>rúguǒ</rt></ruby>“<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>结论<rt>jiélùn</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>评论<rt>pínglùn</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>适用<rt>shìyòng</rt></ruby><ruby>于<rt>yú</rt></ruby>“<ruby>就是<rt>jiùshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后面<rt>hòumian</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>例子<rt>lìzi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>那么<rt>nàme</rt></ruby><ruby>它<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>一定<rt>yídìng</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>适用<rt>shìyòng</rt></ruby><ruby>于<rt>yú</rt></ruby>“<ruby>别说<rt>biéshuō</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后面<rt>hòumian</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>例子<rt>lìzi</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
Here “别说 (biéshuō)” means “let alone / needless to say”, “就是 (jiùshì)” introduces a hypothetical concession (“even”), and “也 (yě)” introduces the conclusion. The example after “就是 (jiùshì)” is used to emphasise the one after “别说 (biéshuō)”: logically, if the conclusion after “也 (yě)” holds for the 就是 (jiùshì)-example, it must also hold for the 别说 (biéshuō)-example. For example:

> **Pattern:** <ruby>别说<rt>biéshuō</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>明显<rt>míngxiǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby>），<ruby>就是<rt>jiùshì</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>更<rt>gèng</rt></ruby><ruby>极端<rt>jíduān</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>／<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby> + <ruby>结论<rt>jiélùn</rt></ruby>  
> never mind (the obvious case); even (the extreme case) + 也 (yě) + conclusion

1. <ruby>别说<rt>Biéshuō</rt></ruby><ruby>写<rt>xiě</rt></ruby><ruby>汉字<rt>Hànzì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>就是<rt>jiùshì</rt></ruby><ruby>读<rt>dú</rt></ruby>，<ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby>。  
   Never mind writing characters — he can't even read them.

2. <ruby>别说<rt>Biéshuō</rt></ruby><ruby>大人<rt>dàrén</rt></ruby>，<ruby>就是<rt>jiùshì</rt></ruby><ruby>小<rt>xiǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>孩子<rt>háizi</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>懂得<rt>dǒngde</rt></ruby><ruby>这个<rt>zhège</rt></ruby><ruby>道理<rt>dàolǐ</rt></ruby>。  
   Not just adults — even small children understand this.

3. <ruby>别说<rt>Biéshuō</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yì</rt></ruby><ruby>天<rt>tiān</rt></ruby>，<ruby>就是<rt>jiùshì</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>星期<rt>xīngqī</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bu</rt></ruby><ruby>完<rt>wán</rt></ruby><ruby>这么<rt>zhème</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>工作<rt>gōngzuò</rt></ruby><ruby>啊<rt>a</rt></ruby>！  
   Forget one day — you couldn't finish this much work even in a week!

> 💡 The 就是 (jiùshì) example must be the harder/more extreme one: kids understanding it proves adults do (✓ 别说大人 (biéshuō dàren)，就是小孩儿也明白 (jiùshì xiǎoháir yě míngbai)); a week not being enough proves a day isn't (✓ 别说一天 (biéshuō yìtiān)，就是一个星期也做不完 (jiùshì yí ge xīngqī yě zuò bu wán)).

### 2. 差点儿 (chàdiǎnr) — The usage of 差点儿 (chàdiǎnr)

<ruby>副词<rt>Fùcí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>差点儿<rt>chàdiǎnr</rt></ruby>”<ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>以下<rt>yǐxià</rt></ruby><ruby>三种<rt>sānzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>用法<rt>yòngfǎ</rt></ruby>：  
The adverb “差点儿 (chàdiǎnr)” has three uses:

1）<ruby>表示<rt>Biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>某种<rt>mǒuzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>希望<rt>xīwàng</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>接近<rt>jiējìn</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>实际<rt>shíjì</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>动词<rt>Dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>肯定<rt>kěndìng</rt></ruby><ruby>式<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>否定<rt>fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>式<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>相同<rt>xiāngtóng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>如<rt>Rú</rt></ruby>：  
1) Something you did NOT want nearly happened, but didn't. Positive and negative verb forms mean the same thing here, e.g.,

1. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>差点儿<rt>chàdiǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>迟<rt>chí</rt></ruby><ruby>到<rt>dào</rt></ruby>。  
   I was almost late. (but I wasn't)

2. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>差点儿<rt>chàdiǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>摔倒<rt>shuāidǎo</rt></ruby>。  
   I nearly fell over. (but I didn't — same meaning as 差点儿摔倒 (chàdiǎnr shuāidǎo))

2）<ruby>表示<rt>Biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>某种<rt>mǒuzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>希望<rt>xīwàng</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>似乎<rt>sìhū</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>终于<rt>zhōngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。<ruby>动词<rt>Dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>否定<rt>fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>式<rt>shì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
2) Something you WANTED seemed not to be going to happen, but in the end it did. Use the negative verb form, e.g.,

3. <ruby>差点儿<rt>Chàdiǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>拿到<rt>nádào</rt></ruby><ruby>冠军<rt>guànjūn</rt></ruby>。  
   (We) very nearly didn't win the championship. (but we did)

4. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>差点儿<rt>chàdiǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>赶上<rt>gǎnshàng</rt></ruby><ruby>飞机<rt>fēijī</rt></ruby>。  
   I almost missed the plane. (but I caught it)

3）<ruby>表示<rt>Biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>某种<rt>mǒuzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>希望<rt>xīwàng</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>接近<rt>jiējìn</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>实际上<rt>shíjìshàng</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>动词<rt>Dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>肯定<rt>kěndìng</rt></ruby><ruby>式<rt>shì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>前面<rt>qiánmiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>常用<rt>chángyòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby>”。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
3) Something you WANTED nearly happened, but didn't. Use the positive verb form, often with “就 (jiù)” before it, e.g.,

5. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>差点儿<rt>chàdiǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>赶上<rt>gǎnshàng</rt></ruby><ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>班<rt>bān</rt></ruby><ruby>航班<rt>hángbān</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   I very nearly caught this flight. (but I missed it)

6. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>差点儿<rt>chàdiǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>赢<rt>yíng</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   I almost beat him. (but I didn't)

> 💡 Rule of thumb: for something good, 差点儿没 (chàdiǎnr méi) + verb = it did happen (phew!); 差点儿就 (chàdiǎnr jiù) + verb = it didn't (so close!). For something bad, 差点儿 (chàdiǎnr) and 差点儿没 (chàdiǎnr méi) both mean it didn't happen. Never 差点儿不 (chàdiǎnr bù) + verb.

### 3. 我说呢 (wǒ shuō ne) — The usage of 我说呢 (wǒ shuō ne)

<ruby>日常<rt>Rìcháng</rt></ruby><ruby>口语<rt>kǒuyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>表达<rt>biǎodá</rt></ruby><ruby>结构<rt>jiégòu</rt></ruby>。<ruby>是<rt>Shì</rt></ruby><ruby>说话<rt>shuōhuà</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby><ruby>最终<rt>zuìzhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby><ruby>一件<rt>yíjiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>缘由<rt>yuányóu</rt></ruby><ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby><ruby>发出<rt>fāchū</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>感慨<rt>gǎnkǎi</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
An everyday spoken expression: what you exclaim when you finally learn the reason for something (“No wonder! / So that's why!”), e.g.,

A: <ruby>小明<rt>Xiǎomíng</rt></ruby><ruby>出<rt>chū</rt></ruby><ruby>国<rt>guó</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
Xiaoming has gone abroad.

B: <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby><ruby>呢<rt>ne</rt></ruby>！<ruby>最近<rt>Zuìjìn</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>看见<rt>kànjiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby>。  
No wonder I haven't seen him lately!

A: <ruby>她<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>对<rt>duì</rt></ruby><ruby>鸡蛋<rt>jīdàn</rt></ruby><ruby>过敏<rt>guòmǐn</rt></ruby>。  
She's allergic to eggs.

B: <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby><ruby>呢<rt>ne</rt></ruby>！<ruby>一起<rt>Yìqǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby><ruby>饭<rt>fàn</rt></ruby><ruby>时<rt>shí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>她<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>从来<rt>cónglái</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby><ruby>鸡蛋<rt>jīdàn</rt></ruby>。  
That explains it! She never eats eggs when we eat together.

> 💡 After 我说呢 (wǒ shuō ne), mention the thing that puzzled you before and now makes sense (often with 怪不得 (guàibude)): ✓ 我说呢 (wǒ shuō ne)，怪不得这几天没看见他 (guàibude zhè jǐtiān méi kànjiàn tā). ✗ 我说呢 (wǒ shuō ne)，他常常生病 (tā chángcháng shēngbìng) (that doesn't connect a reason to an earlier puzzle).
