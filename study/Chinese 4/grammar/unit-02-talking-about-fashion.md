# Chinese 4 · Unit 2: 谈时尚 (Tán shíshàng) — Talking about Fashion

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 33, 39–40, 42–45 (printed pp. 17, 23–24, 26–29)

This unit is about hairstyles, clothes and what's in fashion. Grammar: (1) ……，再说…… (zàishuō……) “…, and besides…”; (2) 看来 (kànlái) “it seems / looks like” (a judgement from what you observe); (3) 虽说…… (suīshuō……)，可是…… (kěshì……) “although…, still…”. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 重点语句 (Zhòngdiǎn yǔjù) — Key sentences

1. <ruby>再说<rt>Zàishuō</rt></ruby><ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>děi</rt></ruby><ruby>烫<rt>tàng</rt></ruby><ruby>发<rt>fà</rt></ruby><ruby>吧<rt>ba</rt></ruby>？  
   Besides, that would need a perm, wouldn't it?

2. <ruby>看来<rt>Kànlái</rt></ruby><ruby>流行<rt>liúxíng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>一定<rt>yídìng</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>适合<rt>shìhé</rt></ruby><ruby>自己<rt>zìjǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   It seems what's fashionable isn't necessarily what suits you.

3. <ruby>虽说<rt>Suīshuō</rt></ruby><ruby>黑色<rt>hēisè</rt></ruby><ruby>和<rt>hé</rt></ruby><ruby>白色<rt>báisè</rt></ruby><ruby>经典<rt>jīngdiǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>过<rt>guò</rt></ruby><ruby>时<rt>shí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可是<rt>kěshì</rt></ruby><ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>总<rt>zǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>穿<rt>chuān</rt></ruby><ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>两<rt>liǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>颜色<rt>yánsè</rt></ruby><ruby>啊<rt>a</rt></ruby>。  
   Black and white may be classics that never go out of style, but you can't wear only those two colours all the time.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. ……，再说…… (……, zàishuō……) — The conjunction ……，再说…… (zàishuō……)

<ruby>连词<rt>Liáncí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>再说<rt>zàishuō</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用于<rt>yòngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>引出<rt>yǐnchū</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>新<rt>xīn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>原因<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>理由<rt>lǐyóu</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The conjunction “再说 (zàishuō)” introduces an additional reason, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>理由<rt>lǐyóu</rt></ruby><ruby>1<rt>yī</rt></ruby>，<ruby>再说<rt>zàishuō</rt></ruby> + <ruby>理由<rt>lǐyóu</rt></ruby><ruby>2<rt>èr</rt></ruby>  
> reason 1, and besides + reason 2

1. <ruby>去<rt>Qù</rt></ruby><ruby>中国<rt>Zhōngguó</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>了解<rt>liǎojiě</rt></ruby><ruby>中国<rt>Zhōngguó</rt></ruby><ruby>文化<rt>wénhuà</rt></ruby>，<ruby>再说<rt>zàishuō</rt></ruby><ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>提高<rt>tígāo</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>口语<rt>kǒuyǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>水平<rt>shuǐpíng</rt></ruby>。  
   Going to China lets me get to know Chinese culture — and besides, it'll improve my spoken Chinese.

2. <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>件<rt>jiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>衣服<rt>yīfu</rt></ruby><ruby>颜色<rt>yánsè</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>漂亮<rt>piàoliang</rt></ruby>，<ruby>再说<rt>zàishuō</rt></ruby><ruby>价钱<rt>jiàqián</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>贵<rt>guì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>买了<rt>mǎile</rt></ruby><ruby>吧<rt>ba</rt></ruby>。  
   This top is a lovely colour, and besides it isn't expensive — buy it.

> 💡 再说 (zàishuō) starts the new reason (✓ 再说价钱也不贵 (zàishuō jiàqian yě bú guì)); it can't go inside the clause (✗ 价钱也不再说贵 (jiàqian yě búzài shuō guì)).

### 2. 看来 (kànlái) — The usage of 看来 (kànlái)

“<ruby>看来<rt>Kànlái</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>通过<rt>tōngguò</rt></ruby><ruby>对<rt>duì</rt></ruby><ruby>某些<rt>mǒuxiē</rt></ruby><ruby>现象<rt>xiànxiàng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>观察<rt>guānchá</rt></ruby>、<ruby>分析<rt>fēnxī</rt></ruby>，<ruby>做出<rt>zuòchū</rt></ruby><ruby>大概<rt>dàgài</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>判断<rt>pànduàn</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>估计<rt>gūjì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>常用<rt>Chángyòng</rt></ruby><ruby>于<rt>yú</rt></ruby><ruby>口语<rt>kǒuyǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>插入语<rt>chārùyǔ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“看来 (kànlái)” is a spoken parenthesis meaning you've made a rough judgement or estimate from observing and analysing something, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>观察<rt>guānchá</rt></ruby><ruby>到<rt>dào</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby>），<ruby>看来<rt>kànlái</rt></ruby> + <ruby>判断<rt>pànduàn</rt></ruby>  
> (what you've noticed), it seems + (your conclusion)

1. <ruby>他<rt>Tā</rt></ruby><ruby>总<rt>zǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>穿<rt>chuān</rt></ruby><ruby>黑色<rt>hēisè</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>衣服<rt>yīfu</rt></ruby>，<ruby>看来<rt>kànlái</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>喜欢<rt>xǐhuan</rt></ruby><ruby>黑色<rt>hēisè</rt></ruby>。  
   He always wears black — he must really like black.

2. <ruby>都<rt>Dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>这么<rt>zhème</rt></ruby><ruby>晚<rt>wǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>看来<rt>kànlái</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>回来<rt>huílai</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   It's so late already — looks like he's not coming back.

> 💡 看来 (kànlái) goes in front of the whole conclusion (often right after the comma): ✓ 看来她一点儿也不喜欢吃辣的菜 (kànlái tā yìdiǎnr yě bù xǐhuan chī là de cài). ✗ 她一点儿也不看来喜欢… (tā yìdiǎnr yě bú kànlái xǐhuan…), ✗ 你很看来喜欢黑色 (nǐ hěn kànlái xǐhuan hēisè).

### 3. 转折复句：虽说……，可是…… (zhuǎnzhé fùjù: suīshuō……, kěshì……) — Transitional compound sentence: 虽说…… (suīshuō……)，可是…… (kěshì……)

<ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby>“<ruby>虽说<rt>suīshuō</rt></ruby>……<ruby>可是<rt>kěshì</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>复句<rt>fùjù</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>承认<rt>chéngrèn</rt></ruby>“<ruby>虽说<rt>suīshuō</rt></ruby>”<ruby>后面<rt>hòumian</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>为<rt>wèi</rt></ruby><ruby>事实<rt>shìshí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby>“<ruby>可是<rt>kěshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>引出<rt>yǐnchū</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>并<rt>bìng</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>因此<rt>yīncǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>成立<rt>chénglì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
In “虽说……可是…… (suīshuō……kěshì……)”, what follows “虽说 (suīshuō)” is accepted as true, but that doesn't cancel out what follows “可是 (kěshì)”, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>虽说<rt>suīshuō</rt></ruby> + <ruby>事实<rt>shìshí</rt></ruby>A，<ruby>可是<rt>kěshì</rt></ruby> + <ruby>事实<rt>shìshí</rt></ruby>B（<ruby>与<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby>A<ruby>相反<rt>xiāngfǎn</rt></ruby>）  
> although A (true), still B (contrary to what A suggests)

1. <ruby>虽说<rt>Suīshuō</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>喜欢<rt>xǐhuan</rt></ruby><ruby>红色<rt>hóngsè</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可是<rt>kěshì</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>穿<rt>chuān</rt></ruby><ruby>红色<rt>hóngsè</rt></ruby><ruby>衣服<rt>yīfu</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>好看<rt>hǎokàn</rt></ruby>。  
   Although I love red, I don't look good in red clothes.

2. <ruby>虽说<rt>Suīshuō</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>已经<rt>yǐjīng</rt></ruby><ruby>70<rt>qī shí</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>岁<rt>suì</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可是<rt>kěshì</rt></ruby><ruby>走<rt>zǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>起<rt>qǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>路<rt>lù</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lai</rt></ruby><ruby>比<rt>bǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>年轻人<rt>niánqīngrén</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>快<rt>kuài</rt></ruby>。  
   Although he's over 70, he walks faster than young people.

> 💡 The two halves must contrast: ✓ 虽说这个发型很流行 (suīshuō zhège fàxíng hěn liúxíng)，可是一点儿也不适合我 (kěshì yìdiǎnr yě bú shìhé wǒ). ✗ …，可是很适合我 (kěshì hěn shìhé wǒ) (no contrast — just use 而且 (érqiě)).
