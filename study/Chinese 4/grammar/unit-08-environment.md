# Chinese 4 · Unit 8: 环境 (Huánjìng) — Environment

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 126, 131–133, 136–139 (printed pp. 111, 116–118, 121–124)

This unit is about pollution and protecting the environment. Grammar: (1) 再加上 (zài jiāshàng) “on top of that / added to which”; (2) 幸亏 (xìngkuī) “fortunately, luckily”. The self-test also checks where 只 (zhǐ) “only” goes. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 再加上 (zài jiāshàng) — The usage of 再加上 (zài jiāshàng)

“<ruby>再<rt>Zài</rt></ruby><ruby>加上<rt>jiāshàng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>“<ruby>此外<rt>cǐwài</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>用于<rt>yòngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>两<rt>liǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>小<rt>xiǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>句<rt>jù</rt></ruby><ruby>之间<rt>zhījiān</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>追述<rt>zhuīshù</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“再加上 (zài jiāshàng)” means “in addition / on top of that”; it goes between two clauses to add a further reason, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>原因<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby><ruby>1<rt>yī</rt></ruby>，<ruby>再<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>加上<rt>jiāshàng</rt></ruby> + <ruby>原因<rt>yuányīn</rt></ruby><ruby>2<rt>èr</rt></ruby>，（<ruby>所以<rt>suǒyǐ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>结果<rt>jiéguǒ</rt></ruby>  
> reason 1, on top of that + reason 2, (so) + result

1. <ruby>最近<rt>Zuìjìn</rt></ruby><ruby>作业<rt>zuòyè</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby>，<ruby>再<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>加上<rt>jiāshàng</rt></ruby><ruby>快<rt>kuài</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>考<rt>kǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>试<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>每<rt>měi</rt></ruby><ruby>天<rt>tiān</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>特别<rt>tèbié</rt></ruby><ruby>忙<rt>máng</rt></ruby>。  
   There's lots of homework lately, and on top of that exams are coming — I'm extremely busy every day.

2. <ruby>最近<rt>Zuìjìn</rt></ruby><ruby>天气<rt>tiānqì</rt></ruby><ruby>比较<rt>bǐjiào</rt></ruby><ruby>冷<rt>lěng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>再<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>加上<rt>jiāshàng</rt></ruby><ruby>穿<rt>chuān</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>太<rt>tài</rt></ruby><ruby>少<rt>shǎo</rt></ruby>，<ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>感冒<rt>gǎnmào</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   It's been cold lately, and he wasn't wearing enough, so he caught a cold.

> 💡 再加上 (zài jiāshàng) starts the second reason; both reasons must push towards the same result: ✓ 有些工厂排放污水 (yǒuxiē gōngchǎng páifàng wūshuǐ)，再加上河边垃圾越来越多 (zài jiāshàng hébiān lājī yuèláiyuè duō)，就变成这样了 (jiù biànchéng zhèyàng le). ✗ …孩子病了再加上 (háizi bìng le zài jiāshàng).

### 2. 幸亏 (xìngkuī) — The usage of 幸亏 (xìngkuī)

<ruby>副词<rt>Fùcí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>幸亏<rt>xìngkuī</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>由于<rt>yóuyú</rt></ruby><ruby>某些<rt>mǒuxiē</rt></ruby><ruby>有利条件<rt>yǒulìtiáojiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>别人<rt>biéren</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>帮助<rt>bāngzhù</rt></ruby><ruby>而<rt>ér</rt></ruby><ruby>避免<rt>bìmiǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>希望<rt>xīwàng</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>后果<rt>hòuguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>得到<rt>dédào</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby><ruby>某种<rt>mǒuzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>好处<rt>hǎochù</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The adverb “幸亏 (xìngkuī)” means that thanks to some favourable circumstance or someone's help, a bad outcome was avoided or something good was gained, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>糟糕<rt>zāogāo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby>），<ruby>幸亏<rt>xìngkuī</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>条件<rt>tiáojiàn</rt></ruby>／<ruby>帮助<rt>bāngzhù</rt></ruby>）  
> (problem), fortunately + (the thing that saved you)

1. <ruby>雨<rt>Yǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>下<rt>xià</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>真<rt>zhēn</rt></ruby><ruby>大<rt>dà</rt></ruby>，<ruby>幸亏<rt>xìngkuī</rt></ruby><ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby><ruby>接<rt>jiē</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   It's pouring — luckily you came to pick me up.

2. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>差点儿<rt>chàdiǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>来晚<rt>láiwǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>幸亏<rt>xìngkuī</rt></ruby><ruby>今天<rt>jīntiān</rt></ruby><ruby>路上<rt>lùshang</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>堵<rt>dǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>车<rt>chē</rt></ruby>。  
   I was nearly late — fortunately there was no traffic today.

3. <ruby>这里<rt>Zhèli</rt></ruby><ruby>环境<rt>huánjìng</rt></ruby><ruby>污染<rt>wūrǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>问题<rt>wèntí</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>严重<rt>yánzhòng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>幸亏<rt>xìngkuī</rt></ruby><ruby>这些<rt>zhèxiē</rt></ruby><ruby>年<rt>nián</rt></ruby><ruby>人们<rt>rénmen</rt></ruby><ruby>意识<rt>yìshí</rt></ruby><ruby>到了<rt>dàole</rt></ruby><ruby>环保<rt>huánbǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>重要性<rt>zhòngyàoxìng</rt></ruby>。  
   Pollution here is a serious problem — fortunately people have realised the importance of environmental protection in recent years.

> 💡 What follows 幸亏 (xìngkuī) must be good news that helps: ✓ 幸亏我的发票还在 (xìngkuī wǒ de fāpiào hái zài)，可以换一个新的 (kěyǐ huàn yí ge xīn de). ✗ 幸亏我没有发票 (xìngkuī wǒ méiyǒu fāpiào)，不能换新的 (bù néng huàn xīn de).

### 3. “只”的位置 (“zhǐ” de wèizhi) — Where 只 (zhǐ) goes

> 💡 From the self-test (result B): 只 (zhǐ) is an adverb, so it goes before the verb as an adverbial.

1. <ruby>有<rt>Yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby><ruby>只<rt>zhǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>关心<rt>guānxīn</rt></ruby><ruby>经济<rt>jīngjì</rt></ruby><ruby>发展<rt>fāzhǎn</rt></ruby>。  
   Some people care only about economic development. (✗ 有的人关心只经济发展 (yǒu de rén guānxīn zhǐ jīngjì fāzhǎn))
