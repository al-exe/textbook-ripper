# Chinese 4 · Unit 3: 售后服务 (Shòuhòufúwù) — After-sale Services

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 47, 53–55, 58–61 (printed pp. 31, 37–39, 42–45)

This unit is about returning faulty goods and complaining politely. Grammar: (1) 突然 (tūrán) “suddenly / sudden”; (2) 即便……也 (jíbiàn……yě)/还是…… (háishi……) “even if…”; (3) ……得住 (de zhù) / ……不住 (buzhù) (can / can't hold, last or stand); (4) 要不然 (yàobùrán) “otherwise”. ## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 突然 (tūrán) — The usage of 突然 (tūrán)

“<ruby>突然<rt>Tūrán</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>形容词<rt>xíngróngcí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>发生<rt>fāshēng</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>急促<rt>jícù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>忽然<rt>hūrán</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The adjective “突然 (tūrán)” means something happened very quickly or suddenly, e.g.,

1. <ruby>突然<rt>Tūrán</rt></ruby><ruby>下起了<rt>xiàqǐle</rt></ruby><ruby>大雨<rt>dàyǔ</rt></ruby>。  
   Suddenly it started pouring with rain.

2. <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>件<rt>jiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>事<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>让<rt>ràng</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>觉得<rt>juéde</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>突然<rt>tūrán</rt></ruby>。  
   This came as a real surprise to him.

> 💡 突然 (tūrán) works as an adverb before a verb (突然坏了 (tūrán huài le) “suddenly broke”) and as an adjective (很突然 (hěn tūrán) “very sudden”) — unlike 忽然 (hūrán), which is only an adverb.

### 2. 即便 (jíbiàn) — The usage of 即便 (jíbiàn)

<ruby>连词<rt>Liáncí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>即便<rt>jíbiàn</rt></ruby>”<ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>“<ruby>即使<rt>jíshǐ</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>假设<rt>jiǎshè</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>让步<rt>ràngbù</rt></ruby>。<ruby>即<rt>Jí</rt></ruby><ruby>承认<rt>chéngrèn</rt></ruby><ruby>某种<rt>mǒuzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>事实<rt>shìshí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>暂<rt>zàn</rt></ruby><ruby>让<rt>ràng</rt></ruby><ruby>一步<rt>yíbù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>正<rt>zhèng</rt></ruby><ruby>句<rt>jù</rt></ruby><ruby>里<rt>li</rt></ruby><ruby>常用<rt>chángyòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>”<ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby>“<ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby>”<ruby>呼应<rt>hūyìng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>说出<rt>shuōchū</rt></ruby><ruby>结论<rt>jiélùn</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The conjunction “即便 (jíbiàn)” means “即使 (jíshǐ)” (even if) — a hypothetical concession: you grant something for the sake of argument, and the main clause, usually with “也 (yě)” or “还是 (háishi)”, gives the conclusion, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>即便<rt>jíbiàn</rt></ruby> + <ruby>假设<rt>jiǎshè</rt></ruby>，（<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby>／<ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby> + <ruby>结论<rt>jiélùn</rt></ruby>  
> even if + (supposition), (subject) + still + (conclusion)

1. <ruby>太<rt>Tài</rt></ruby><ruby>晚<rt>wǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>即便<rt>jíbiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>马上<rt>mǎshàng</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby>，<ruby>到<rt>dào</rt></ruby><ruby>那儿<rt>nàr</rt></ruby><ruby>银行<rt>yínháng</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>关<rt>guān</rt></ruby><ruby>门<rt>mén</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   It's too late — even if you go right now, the bank will be closed when you get there.

2. <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>篇<rt>piān</rt></ruby><ruby>文章<rt>wénzhāng</rt></ruby><ruby>太<rt>tài</rt></ruby><ruby>难<rt>nán</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>即便<rt>jíbiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>再<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>读<rt>dú</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>遍<rt>biàn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby><ruby>读<rt>dú</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bu</rt></ruby><ruby>懂<rt>dǒng</rt></ruby>。  
   This article is too hard — even if I read it again, I still won't understand it.

> 💡 Pair 即便 (jíbiàn) with 也 (yě) or 还是 (háishi), not 就 (jiù): ✓ 即便想赢 (jíbiàn xiǎng yíng)，也赢不了 (yě yíng buliǎo); ✓ 即便说错了 (jíbiàn shuōcuò le)，也不用不好意思 (yě bú yòng bùhǎoyìsi).

### 3. ……得住、……不住 (…… de zhù, …… bu zhù) — The usage of ……得住 (de zhù) and ……不住 (buzhù)

“……<ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>住<rt>zhù</rt></ruby>”<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>后<rt>hòu</rt></ruby>，<ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>可能<rt>kěnéng</rt></ruby><ruby>补语<rt>bǔyǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>牢固<rt>láogù</rt></ruby>、<ruby>稳当<rt>wěndang</rt></ruby>，<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>力量<rt>lìliang</rt></ruby><ruby>够<rt>gòu</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shàng</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>持续<rt>chíxù</rt></ruby><ruby>较<rt>jiào</rt></ruby><ruby>长<rt>cháng</rt></ruby><ruby>时间<rt>shíjiān</rt></ruby>，<ruby>否定<rt>fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>形式<rt>xíngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>“……<ruby>不<rt>bu</rt></ruby><ruby>住<rt>zhù</rt></ruby>”。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“……得住 (de zhù)” follows a verb as a potential complement, meaning firm and steady, strong enough, or able to last; its negative form is “……不住 (buzhù)”, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby> + <ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>住<rt>zhù</rt></ruby>（<ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby>）／<ruby>不<rt>bu</rt></ruby><ruby>住<rt>zhù</rt></ruby>（<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby>）  
> verb + 得住 (de zhù) (can hold / last) / 不住 (buzhù) (can't)

1. <ruby>这<rt>Zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>种<rt>zhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>台灯<rt>táidēng</rt></ruby><ruby>虽然<rt>suīrán</rt></ruby><ruby>比较<rt>bǐjiào</rt></ruby><ruby>贵<rt>guì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>但是<rt>dànshì</rt></ruby><ruby>质量<rt>zhìliàng</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby>，<ruby>用<rt>yòng</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>住<rt>zhù</rt></ruby>。  
   This lamp is a bit pricey, but it's good quality — it'll last.

2. <ruby>这么<rt>Zhème</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>东西<rt>dōngxi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>拿<rt>ná</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>住<rt>zhù</rt></ruby><ruby>拿<rt>ná</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bu</rt></ruby><ruby>住<rt>zhù</rt></ruby>？  
   So many things — can you hold them all?

3. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>累<rt>lèi</rt></ruby><ruby>得<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>站<rt>zhàn</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bu</rt></ruby><ruby>住<rt>zhù</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   I'm so tired I can't stand up any more.

> 💡 Negate inside the verb: 用不住 (yòng buzhù), 拿不住 (nábuzhù) — never ✗ 不用住 (bú yòng zhù). 拿不住了 (nábuzhù le) = “I can't hold on any longer” (so: 快帮帮我 (kuài bāngbang wǒ)！).

### 4. 要不然 (yàobùrán) — The usage of 要不然 (yàobùrán)

“<ruby>要不然<rt>Yàobùrán</rt></ruby>”<ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby>“<ruby>如果<rt>rúguǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>这样<rt>zhèyàng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>否则<rt>fǒuzé</rt></ruby>”，<ruby>用来<rt>yònglái</rt></ruby><ruby>引出<rt>yǐnchū</rt></ruby><ruby>与<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shàng</rt></ruby><ruby>文<rt>wén</rt></ruby><ruby>相反<rt>xiāngfǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby><ruby>并<rt>bìng</rt></ruby><ruby>加以<rt>jiāyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>阐述<rt>chǎnshù</rt></ruby>。<ruby>多<rt>Duō</rt></ruby><ruby>用于<rt>yòngyú</rt></ruby><ruby>口语<rt>kǒuyǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>“<ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>”。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“要不然 (yàobùrán)” means “if not / otherwise”: it introduces what would happen in the opposite case. Mostly spoken; you can also say “要不 (yào bù)”, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>建议<rt>jiànyì</rt></ruby>／<ruby>事实<rt>shìshí</rt></ruby>，<ruby>要不然<rt>yàobùrán</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>好<rt>hǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>）<ruby>结果<rt>jiéguǒ</rt></ruby>  
> advice / fact, otherwise + (bad) result

1. <ruby>快<rt>Kuài</rt></ruby><ruby>点儿<rt>diǎnr</rt></ruby><ruby>吧<rt>ba</rt></ruby>，<ruby>要不然<rt>yàobùrán</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby><ruby>迟到<rt>chídào</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   Hurry up, or we'll be late.

2. <ruby>我<rt>Wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>可能<rt>kěnéng</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>病<rt>bìng</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>要不然<rt>yàobùrán</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   I think he must be ill — otherwise he wouldn't miss it.

> 💡 要不然 (yàobùrán) starts the second clause, after the advice: ✓ 小票你得留好 (xiǎopiào nǐ de liú hǎo)，要不然就不能退货了 (yàobùrán jiù bù néng tuìhuò le) — ✗ 要不然小票你得留好 (yàobùrán xiǎopiào nǐ de liú hǎo)，…
