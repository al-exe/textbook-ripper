# Chinese 4 · Unit 7: 谈私家车 (Tán sījiāchē) — Talking about Private Cars

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 111, 117–118, 121–124 (printed pp. 95, 101–102, 105–108)

This unit is about owning a car — costs, traffic, freedom. Grammar: (1) 早知道…… (zǎo zhīdào……)，还不如…… (hái bùrú……) “if I'd known…, I'd rather have…” (regret); (2) 想……就…… (xiǎng……jiù……) “do whatever you like”; (3) 反正 (fǎnzhèng) “anyway, in any case”. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 重点语句 (Zhòngdiǎn yǔjù) — Key sentences

1. <ruby>早<rt>Zǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby><ruby>这样<rt>zhèyàng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>咱们<rt>zánmen</rt></ruby><ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby><ruby>坐<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>地铁<rt>dìtiě</rt></ruby><ruby>呢<rt>ne</rt></ruby>。  
   If we'd known it would be like this, we'd have been better off taking the subway.

2. <ruby>买<rt>Mǎi</rt></ruby><ruby>辆<rt>liàng</rt></ruby><ruby>车<rt>chē</rt></ruby>，<ruby>周末<rt>zhōumò</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>时候<rt>shíhou</rt></ruby><ruby>咱们<rt>zánmen</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>哪儿<rt>nǎr</rt></ruby><ruby>玩儿<rt>wánr</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>哪儿<rt>nǎr</rt></ruby><ruby>玩儿<rt>wánr</rt></ruby>。

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 早知道……，还不如…… (zǎo zhīdào……, hái bùrú……) — The usage of 早知道…… (zǎo zhīdào……)，还不如…… (hái bùrú……)

<ruby>该<rt>Gāi</rt></ruby><ruby>句型<rt>jùxíng</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>一种<rt>yìzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>虚拟语气<rt>xūnǐyǔqì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>表明<rt>biǎomíng</rt></ruby><ruby>说话<rt>shuōhuà</rt></ruby><ruby>者<rt>zhě</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>后悔<rt>hòuhuǐ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
This pattern expresses the subjunctive mood: the speaker deeply regrets something, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>早<rt>zǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>想到<rt>xiǎngdào</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby>），（<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>本来<rt>běnlái</rt></ruby><ruby>应该<rt>yīnggāi</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事<rt>shì</rt></ruby>）  
> if I'd known + (what turned out), (subject) + would rather have + (the better choice)

1. <ruby>早<rt>Zǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby><ruby>这个<rt>zhège</rt></ruby><ruby>电影<rt>diànyǐng</rt></ruby><ruby>这么<rt>zhème</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>家<rt>jiā</rt></ruby><ruby>睡<rt>shuì</rt></ruby><ruby>觉<rt>jiào</rt></ruby>。  
   If I'd known this film was so boring, I'd have stayed home and slept.

2. <ruby>早<rt>Zǎo</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby><ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>家<rt>jiā</rt></ruby><ruby>饭馆<rt>fànguǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>菜<rt>cài</rt></ruby><ruby>这么<rt>zhème</rt></ruby><ruby>难吃<rt>nánchī</rt></ruby>，<ruby>咱们<rt>zánmen</rt></ruby><ruby>还<rt>hái</rt></ruby><ruby>不如<rt>bùrú</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>别<rt>bié</rt></ruby><ruby>家<rt>jiā</rt></ruby>。  
   If we'd known the food here was this bad, we'd have gone somewhere else.

> 💡 早知道 (zǎo zhīdào) starts the sentence; 还不如 (hái bùrú) goes right before the better option: ✓ 早知道这个电影这么无聊 (zǎo zhīdào zhège diànyǐng zhème wúliáo)，还不如在家看电视 (hái bùrú zàijiā kàndiànshì). ✗ 这个电影早知道… (zhège diànyǐng zǎo zhīdào…)，在家还不如看电视 (zàijiā hái bùrú kàndiànshì).

### 2. 想……就…… (xiǎng…… jiù……) — The usage of 想……就…… (xiǎng……jiù……)

<ruby>该<rt>Gāi</rt></ruby><ruby>句型<rt>jùxíng</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>可以<rt>kěyǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>随心所欲<rt>suíxīnsuǒyù</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>某事<rt>mǒushì</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
This pattern means you can do whatever you please, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby> + <ruby>疑问词<rt>yíwèncí</rt></ruby>……<ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby> + <ruby>同<rt>tóng</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>疑问词<rt>yíwèncí</rt></ruby>……  
> want to + verb + where/what/how… then + same verb + same question word (= whatever / wherever)

1. <ruby>放<rt>Fàng</rt></ruby><ruby>假<rt>jià</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>哪儿<rt>nǎr</rt></ruby><ruby>玩儿<rt>wánr</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>哪儿<rt>nǎr</rt></ruby><ruby>玩儿<rt>wánr</rt></ruby>。  
   It's the holidays — go wherever you like.

2. <ruby>要<rt>Yào</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>减<rt>jiǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>肥<rt>féi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>你<rt>nǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>总是<rt>zǒngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>想<rt>xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby><ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>吃<rt>chī</rt></ruby><ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby>。  
   If you want to lose weight, you can't always eat whatever you feel like.

> 💡 Repeat the same verb and question word on both sides: ✓ 想去哪儿就去哪儿 (xiǎng qù nǎr jiù qù nǎr) / ✓ 想怎么去就怎么去 (xiǎng zěnme qù jiù zěnme qù). ✗ 想去哪儿就去我去 (xiǎng qù nǎr jiù qù wǒ qù).

### 3. 反正 (fǎnzhèng) — The usage of 反正 (fǎnzhèng)

“<ruby>反正<rt>Fǎnzhèng</rt></ruby>”<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>不管<rt>bùguǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>什么<rt>shénme</rt></ruby><ruby>条件<rt>tiáojiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>下<rt>xià</rt></ruby>，<ruby>做法<rt>zuòfǎ</rt></ruby>、<ruby>结论<rt>jiélùn</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby><ruby>改变<rt>gǎibiàn</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“反正 (fǎnzhèng)” means that whatever the conditions, the action or conclusion won't change, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>不管<rt>bùguǎn</rt></ruby>……，<ruby>反正<rt>fǎnzhèng</rt></ruby> + <ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>决定<rt>juédìng</rt></ruby>／<ruby>结论<rt>jiélùn</rt></ruby>  
> no matter…, anyway + subject + (decision that won't change)

1. <ruby>不管<rt>Bùguǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>你们<rt>nǐmen</rt></ruby><ruby>怎么<rt>zěnme</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>，<ruby>反正<rt>fǎnzhèng</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>同意<rt>tóngyì</rt></ruby>。  
   Whatever you say, I don't agree anyway.

2. <ruby>不管<rt>Bùguǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>刮<rt>guā</rt></ruby><ruby>风<rt>fēng</rt></ruby><ruby>还是<rt>háishi</rt></ruby><ruby>下<rt>xià</rt></ruby><ruby>雨<rt>yǔ</rt></ruby>，<ruby>反正<rt>fǎnzhèng</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby>。  
   Wind or rain, I'm going anyway.

> 💡 反正 (fǎnzhèng) comes before the subject of the decision, at the start of the clause: ✓ 反正我不会改变主意的 (fǎnzhèng wǒ bú huì gǎibiàn zhǔyi de). ✗ 我不会改变反正主意的 (wǒ bú huì gǎibiàn fǎnzhèng zhǔyi de).
