# Chinese 4 · Unit 6: 网络与手机 (Wǎngluò yǔ shǒujī) — Internet and Mobile Phones

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 95, 101–103, 106–109 (printed pp. 79, 85–87, 90–93)

This unit is about how the internet and smartphones change our lives. Grammar: (1) 自从……以后 (zìcóng……yǐhòu) “ever since…”; (2) 要么……要么…… (yàome……yàome……) “either… or…”; (3) 从某种程度上来说 (cóng mǒuzhǒngchéngdù shàng láishuō) “to some extent”. Also useful: A倒是 (dào shì)A了 (le)，可是…… (kěshì……) “it is A, all right, but…”. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 自从 (zìcóng) — The usage of 自从 (zìcóng)

<ruby>表示<rt>Biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>以<rt>yǐ</rt></ruby><ruby>过去<rt>guòqu</rt></ruby><ruby>某一时间<rt>mǒuyìshíjiān</rt></ruby><ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yí</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>为<rt>wèi</rt></ruby><ruby>起点<rt>qǐdiǎn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>例如<rt>lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“自从 (zìcóng)” takes a past time or event as the starting point (“ever since…”), e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>自从<rt>zìcóng</rt></ruby> + <ruby>过去<rt>guòqù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>时间<rt>shíjiān</rt></ruby>／<ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby> + （<ruby>以后<rt>yǐhòu</rt></ruby>），+ <ruby>一直<rt>yìzhí</rt></ruby>／<ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby>……  
> ever since + (past time/event), + (what has been the case since)

1. <ruby>自从<rt>Zìcóng</rt></ruby><ruby>5<rt>wǔ</rt></ruby><ruby>月份<rt>yuèfèn</rt></ruby><ruby>以后<rt>yǐhòu</rt></ruby>，<ruby>天气<rt>tiānqì</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>渐渐<rt>jiànjiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>暖和<rt>nuǎnhuo</rt></ruby><ruby>起来<rt>qǐlai</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   Since May, the weather has gradually been getting warmer.

2. <ruby>自从<rt>Zìcóng</rt></ruby><ruby>毕<rt>bì</rt></ruby><ruby>业<rt>yè</rt></ruby><ruby>以后<rt>yǐhòu</rt></ruby>，<ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>一直<rt>yìzhí</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>银行<rt>yínháng</rt></ruby><ruby>工作<rt>gōngzuò</rt></ruby>。  
   Ever since graduating, he's worked at a bank.

> 💡 自从 (zìcóng) goes before the time/event, at the start: ✓ 自从上大学以后 (zìcóng shàng dàxué yǐhòu)，我们就很少见面了 (wǒmen jiù hěnshǎo jiànmiàn le). ✗ 上大学自从以后 (shàng dàxué zìcóng yǐhòu) / ✗ 毕业以后 (bìyè yǐhòu)，自从他就… (zìcóng tā jiù…)

### 2. 要么 (yàome) — The usage of 要么 (yàome)

<ruby>表示<rt>Biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>多种<rt>duōzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>情况<rt>qíngkuàng</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>一种<rt>yìzhǒng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>或<rt>huò</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>多种<rt>duōzhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>可能<rt>kěnéng</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>选择<rt>xuǎnzé</rt></ruby><ruby>一种<rt>yìzhǒng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>常用<rt>Chángyòng</rt></ruby>“<ruby>要么<rt>yàome</rt></ruby>……<ruby>要么<rt>yàome</rt></ruby>……”，<ruby>例如<rt>lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
“要么 (yàome)” refers to one of several cases or one choice among several possibilities; usually as “要么……要么…… (yàome……yàome……)”, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** （<ruby>主语<rt>zhǔyǔ</rt></ruby>）+ <ruby>要么<rt>yàome</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>短语<rt>duǎnyǔ</rt></ruby>A，<ruby>要么<rt>yàome</rt></ruby> + <ruby>动词<rt>dòngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>短语<rt>duǎnyǔ</rt></ruby>B  
> (subject) + either + A, or + B — each 要么 (yàome) starts a parallel phrase

1. <ruby>昨天<rt>Zuótiān</rt></ruby><ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>给<rt>gěi</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>打<rt>dǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>电话<rt>diànhuà</rt></ruby>，<ruby>要么<rt>yàome</rt></ruby><ruby>打<rt>dǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bu</rt></ruby><ruby>通<rt>tōng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>要么<rt>yàome</rt></ruby><ruby>没<rt>méi</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby><ruby>接<rt>jiē</rt></ruby>。  
   When I called him yesterday, either I couldn't get through or no one answered.

2. <ruby>不<rt>Bù</rt></ruby><ruby>明白<rt>míngbai</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>地方<rt>dìfang</rt></ruby><ruby>要么<rt>yàome</rt></ruby><ruby>查<rt>chá</rt></ruby><ruby>一下<rt>yíxià</rt></ruby><ruby>书<rt>shū</rt></ruby>，<ruby>要么<rt>yàome</rt></ruby><ruby>问<rt>wèn</rt></ruby><ruby>老师<rt>lǎoshī</rt></ruby>。  
   For anything you don't understand, either look it up or ask the teacher.

> 💡 Both 要么 (yàome) sit in front of matching phrases: ✓ 他每天要么在图书馆学习 (tā měitiān yàome zài túshūguǎn xuéxí)，要么去健身房锻炼 (yàome qù jiànshēnfáng duànliàn). ✗ 他每天在图书馆要么学习 (tā měitiān zài túshūguǎn yàome xuéxí)，… ✗ 要么去公园 (yàome qù gōngyuán)，去博物馆 (qù bówùguǎn)，要么你决定吧 (yàome nǐ juédìng ba).

### 3. 从某种程度上来说 (cóng mǒu zhǒng chéngdù shang lái shuō) — The usage of 从某种程度上来说 (cóng mǒuzhǒngchéngdù shàng láishuō)

<ruby>表示<rt>Biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>一定<rt>yídìng</rt></ruby><ruby>深度<rt>shēndù</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>考量<rt>kǎoliáng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
Introduces a considered point, “to some extent / in a sense”, e.g.,

1. <ruby>从<rt>Cóng</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>种<rt>zhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>程度<rt>chéngdù</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shang</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>，<ruby>网络<rt>wǎngluò</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>拉近<rt>lājìn</rt></ruby><ruby>人们<rt>rénmen</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>zhī</rt></ruby><ruby>间<rt>jiān</rt></ruby><ruby>距离<rt>jùlí</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>同时<rt>tóngshí</rt></ruby><ruby>也<rt>yě</rt></ruby><ruby>让<rt>ràng</rt></ruby><ruby>人们<rt>rénmen</rt></ruby><ruby>疏远<rt>shūyuǎn</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   To some extent, while the internet brings people closer, it also drives them apart.

2. <ruby>从<rt>Cóng</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>种<rt>zhǒng</rt></ruby><ruby>程度<rt>chéngdù</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shang</rt></ruby><ruby>来<rt>lái</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby>，<ruby>我<rt>wǒ</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>上<rt>shàng</rt></ruby><ruby>当<rt>dàng</rt></ruby><ruby>受<rt>shòu</rt></ruby><ruby>骗<rt>piàn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>感觉<rt>gǎnjué</rt></ruby>。  
   In a way, I feel like I've been cheated.

> 💡 It's a whole phrase that goes at the start of the clause: ✓ 从某种程度上来说 (cóng mǒuzhǒngchéngdù shàng láishuō)，适量喝红酒对心脏有好处 (shìliàng hē hóngjiǔ duì xīnzàng yǒu hǎochù). ✗ 适量从某种程度上来说喝红酒… (shìliàng cóng mǒuzhǒngchéngdù shàng láishuō hē hóngjiǔ…)

### 4. A倒是A了，可是…… (A dàoshì A le, kěshì……) — A is A, all right, but…

> 💡 Not a numbered note, but in the key sentences and drill examples: repeat the word around 倒是 (dào shì) to concede a point, then give the downside with 可是 (kěshì).

1. <ruby>方便<rt>Fāngbiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>倒是<rt>dàoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>方便<rt>fāngbiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可是<rt>kěshì</rt></ruby><ruby>这<rt>zhè</rt></ruby><ruby>却<rt>què</rt></ruby><ruby>让<rt>ràng</rt></ruby><ruby>我们<rt>wǒmen</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>距离<rt>jùlí</rt></ruby><ruby>变<rt>biàn</rt></ruby><ruby>大<rt>dà</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>。  
   It's convenient, all right, but it has pushed us apart.

2. <ruby>自从<rt>Zìcóng</rt></ruby><ruby>打<rt>dǎ</rt></ruby><ruby>工<rt>gōng</rt></ruby><ruby>以后<rt>yǐhòu</rt></ruby>，<ruby>倒是<rt>dàoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>钱<rt>qián</rt></ruby><ruby>了<rt>le</rt></ruby>，<ruby>可是<rt>kěshì</rt></ruby><ruby>很<rt>hěn</rt></ruby><ruby>多<rt>duō</rt></ruby><ruby>事情<rt>shìqing</rt></ruby><ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>时间<rt>shíjiān</rt></ruby><ruby>去<rt>qù</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby>。  
   Since I started working part-time I do have money, but no time for lots of things.
