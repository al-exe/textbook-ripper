# Chinese 4 · Unit 9: 地理 (Dìlǐ) — Geography

**Grammar & Sentence Patterns** · source: PDF pp. 141, 147–149, 153–155 (printed pp. 127, 133–135, 139–141)

This unit is about geography — area, population and what places are famous for (using Xinjiang as the example). Grammar: (1) saying fractions and percentages (三分之二 (sānfēnzhī'èr), 百分之六十 (bǎifēnzhīliùshí)); (2) 凡是……都…… (fánshì……dōu……) “every… / all…”; (3) double negatives (没有不…… (méiyǒu bù……), 不……不行 (bù……bùxíng)) for strong positives. Answers marked “model answer” were written for this sheet.

## 语法点注释 (Yǔfǎ diǎn zhùshì) — Grammar notes

### 1. 分数的表达 (fēnshù de biǎodá) — Saying fractions

> **Pattern:** <ruby>分母<rt>fēnmǔ</rt></ruby> + <ruby>分<rt>fēn</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>zhī</rt></ruby> + <ruby>分子<rt>fēnzǐ</rt></ruby>  
> denominator (bottom) + 分之 (fēn zhī) + numerator (top) — the bottom number comes first!

1. 1/2：<ruby>二<rt>èr</rt></ruby><ruby>分<rt>fēn</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>zhī</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yī</rt></ruby>  
   one half

2. 2/3：<ruby>三<rt>sān</rt></ruby><ruby>分<rt>fēn</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>zhī</rt></ruby><ruby>二<rt>èr</rt></ruby>  
   two thirds

3. 1%：<ruby>百<rt>bǎi</rt></ruby><ruby>分<rt>fēn</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>zhī</rt></ruby><ruby>一<rt>yī</rt></ruby>  
   one percent

4. 60%：<ruby>百<rt>bǎi</rt></ruby><ruby>分<rt>fēn</rt></ruby><ruby>之<rt>zhī</rt></ruby><ruby>六十<rt>liùshí</rt></ruby>  
   sixty percent

> 💡 “A makes up … of B” = A 占 (zhàn) B 的 (de) …: 新疆的少数民族占人口总数的百分之六十 (Xīnjiāng de shǎoshùmínzú zhàn rénkǒuzǒngshù de bǎifēnzhīliùshí). ✓ 百分之九十以上 (bǎifēnzhījiǔshí yǐshàng) (over 90%), ✗ 九十百分之 (jiǔshí bǎifēnzhī); ✓ 三分之二 (sānfēnzhī'èr) (2/3), ✗ 二分之三 (èrfēn zhīsān) (that's 3/2).

### 2. 凡是 (fánshì) — The usage of 凡是 (fánshì)

<ruby>副词<rt>Fùcí</rt></ruby>“<ruby>凡是<rt>fánshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby>“<ruby>所有<rt>suǒyǒu</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>意思<rt>yìsi</rt></ruby>，<ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>某<rt>mǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>个<rt>ge</rt></ruby><ruby>范围<rt>fànwéi</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby><ruby>无一例外<rt>wúyílìwài</rt></ruby>。<ruby>在<rt>Zài</rt></ruby>“<ruby>凡是<rt>fánshì</rt></ruby>……，（<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby>）……”<ruby>结构<rt>jiégòu</rt></ruby><ruby>中<rt>zhōng</rt></ruby>，“<ruby>凡是<rt>fánshì</rt></ruby>”<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>后边<rt>hòubian</rt></ruby><ruby>一般<rt>yìbān</rt></ruby><ruby>是<rt>shì</rt></ruby><ruby>名词<rt>míngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>短语<rt>duǎnyǔ</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
The adverb “凡是 (fánshì)” means “all / every”: there's no exception within a certain range. In “凡是…… (fánshì……)，（都 (dōu)）……”, what follows 凡是 (fánshì) is usually a noun phrase, e.g.,

> **Pattern:** <ruby>凡是<rt>fánshì</rt></ruby> + <ruby>名词<rt>míngcí</rt></ruby><ruby>短语<rt>duǎnyǔ</rt></ruby>（……<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>／……<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事<rt>shì</rt></ruby>），<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby> + ……  
> every + (noun phrase, usually ending in 的 (de) + noun), 都 (dōu) + …

1. <ruby>凡是<rt>Fánshì</rt></ruby><ruby>认识<rt>rènshi</rt></ruby><ruby>她<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>，<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>说<rt>shuō</rt></ruby><ruby>她<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>漂亮<rt>piàoliang</rt></ruby>。  
   Everyone who knows her says she's beautiful.

2. <ruby>凡是<rt>Fánshì</rt></ruby><ruby>应该<rt>yīnggāi</rt></ruby><ruby>做<rt>zuò</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>事<rt>shì</rt></ruby>，<ruby>都<rt>dōu</rt></ruby><ruby>要<rt>yào</rt></ruby><ruby>努力<rt>nǔlì</rt></ruby><ruby>做好<rt>zuòhǎo</rt></ruby>。  
   Everything that should be done must be done well.

> 💡 After 凡是 (fánshì) you need a noun phrase, so keep the 的 (de) + noun: ✓ 凡是他写的书 (fánshì tā xiě de shū) / ✓ 凡是去过那儿的人 (fánshì qùguò nàr de rén). ✗ 凡是他写书 (fánshì tā xiě shū) / ✗ 凡是去过那儿 (fánshì qùguò nàr)，…

### 3. 双重否定 (shuāngchóng fǒudìng) — Double negation

<ruby>双重<rt>Shuāngchóng</rt></ruby><ruby>否定<rt>fǒudìng</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>肯定<rt>kěndìng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>有时<rt>yǒushí</rt></ruby><ruby>表示<rt>biǎoshì</rt></ruby><ruby>强调<rt>qiángdiào</rt></ruby>。<ruby>常见<rt>Chángjiàn</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>形式<rt>xíngshì</rt></ruby><ruby>有<rt>yǒu</rt></ruby>“<ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……”“<ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby>……<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……”“<ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby>……<ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby>……”“<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……”<ruby>等<rt>děng</rt></ruby>。<ruby>例如<rt>Lìrú</rt></ruby>：  
A double negative expresses a positive, sometimes with emphasis. Common forms: “没有不…… (méiyǒu bù……)”, “没有……不…… (méiyǒu……bù……)”, “没有……没有…… (méiyǒu……méiyǒu……)”, “不……不…… (bù……bù……)”, e.g.,

1. <ruby>那个<rt>Nàge</rt></ruby><ruby>电影<rt>diànyǐng</rt></ruby><ruby>非常<rt>fēicháng</rt></ruby><ruby>有名<rt>yǒumíng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby>。  
   That film is very famous — there's no one who doesn't know it.

2. <ruby>凡是<rt>Fánshì</rt></ruby><ruby>听过<rt>tīngguo</rt></ruby><ruby>她<rt>tā</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>歌<rt>gē</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby>，<ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>喜欢<rt>xǐhuan</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   Everyone who's heard her songs loves them.

> **Pattern:** A. ……<ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……<ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>　B. <ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby>……<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……　C. <ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby>／<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……<ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby>／<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……　D. <ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby>……<ruby>不行<rt>bùxíng</rt></ruby>  
> A. none who don't… · B. there's no… that doesn't… · C. without… you can't… · D. you must… (not … won't do)

3. <ruby>这个<rt>Zhège</rt></ruby><ruby>歌<rt>gē</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bú</rt></ruby><ruby>会<rt>huì</rt></ruby><ruby>唱<rt>chàng</rt></ruby><ruby>的<rt>de</rt></ruby>。  
   Everyone can sing this song. (A)

4. <ruby>成<rt>Chéng</rt></ruby><ruby>龙<rt>Lóng</rt></ruby><ruby>非常<rt>fēicháng</rt></ruby><ruby>有名<rt>yǒumíng</rt></ruby>，<ruby>在<rt>zài</rt></ruby><ruby>中国<rt>Zhōngguó</rt></ruby><ruby>没有<rt>méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>人<rt>rén</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>知道<rt>zhīdào</rt></ruby><ruby>他<rt>tā</rt></ruby>。  
   Jackie Chan is very famous — everyone in China knows him. (B)

5. <ruby>没有<rt>Méiyǒu</rt></ruby><ruby>护照<rt>hùzhào</rt></ruby><ruby>就<rt>jiù</rt></ruby><ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>能<rt>néng</rt></ruby><ruby>出<rt>chū</rt></ruby><ruby>国<rt>guó</rt></ruby>。  
   Without a passport you can't go abroad. (C)

6. <ruby>想<rt>Xiǎng</rt></ruby><ruby>获得<rt>huòdé</rt></ruby><ruby>冠军<rt>guànjūn</rt></ruby>，<ruby>不<rt>bù</rt></ruby><ruby>努力<rt>nǔlì</rt></ruby><ruby>不行<rt>bùxíng</rt></ruby>。  
   If you want to win, you have to work hard. (D)

> 💡 Both negatives are needed for the positive meaning: ✓ 还没有不喜欢她做的菜的 (hái méiyǒu bù xǐhuan tā zuò de cài de) (everyone likes it). Dropping one (✗ 还没有喜欢她做的菜的 (hái méiyǒu xǐhuan tā zuò de cài de)) means nobody likes it.
